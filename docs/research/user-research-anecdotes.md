## Wendell

**Topic: Shared Universe / "Cosmere Model"**

- Suggested a "Cosmere model" (like Brandon Sanderson's shared universe) would work well
- Players don't have to rebuild game lore from the ground up
- They can use the existing framework to build their own towns, areas, or planets
- Lowers the barrier to creation while maintaining narrative coherence

**Topic: Returning Player Experience**

- When returning to a game after time away, doesn't know what to do and gets lost
- An AI assistant to catch players back up would help
- Could be a collectible and upgradable companion (like a faerie)
- Gives the catch-up mechanic a narrative wrapper that feels like part of the game

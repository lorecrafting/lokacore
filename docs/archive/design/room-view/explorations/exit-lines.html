Exits on one line

Sixteen ways to print the exits the way a MUD does: one line under the room, with the prompt below it at the very bottom (time, place, hp, mana, mv and your position). Every word is a tap target. Use the controls to change the room, your position and how hurt you are. Tap an exit while resting and you’re told to stand first, the way a MUD would.

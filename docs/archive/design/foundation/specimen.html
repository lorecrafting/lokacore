Tap Elspeth to open her page: the leaf curls forward. Leave curls it back. The arriving page takes taps from the first frame; the curl is a picture over it.

The curl is page-curl.sksl, inlined verbatim in this page's source and run by CanvasKit 0.42.0, the same file Skia will run on the phone. This sample's page snapshot skips the minimap drawing. The curl takes 500 ms (to be tuned on a device); reduce motion swaps it for a 160 ms cross-fade. Sound plays the mock's synthesised paper turn until a recorded CC0 sample replaces it. The clock sets in-game time: the page is light from dawn (05:00) to dusk (18:00), the Chapter 1 calendar's sample hours, and dark otherwise.

Not in the base: grain, ink shadows, weather, glow, burns, flashes, pickup pops, chapter cards and every other mock effect.

## Findings

- **N-1 (nit)** `docs/lessons/mobile.md:56`: the documented selector
  `press 'label="Offer to fetch Bram's lantern"'` closes the single quote at the apostrophe.
  Pasted into zsh it fails to parse (`zsh -n`: `unmatched "`), so the next agent's walk stops on its
  first quest step. Write it as `"label=\"Offer to fetch Bram's lantern\""`.
- **Q-1 (question, for the owner's polish batch, not this PR)** The room log shows raw outcome codes
  for commands without narration (`moved`, `choice_opened`, `waited`, `choice_closed`,
  `scanned`; smoke.ts `said`, unchanged here). P5's "readable current state" may want words for
  these; the PM rulings do not cover it.

No over-engineering found: `sayers` and `reread` are split out to keep `openSmoke` under the
function cap; `Act`, `Title`, `Here`, `Choice` split `RoomPage` for the same reason.

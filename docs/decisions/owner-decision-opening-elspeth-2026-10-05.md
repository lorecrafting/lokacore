# Owner direction and PM selection: opening Elspeth — 2026-10-05

The owner, verbatim: “Also we should have an NPC at the opening room, just so new players hav esomebody to talk to and somebody to direct them to the next place”.

The PM selects Elspeth, Wren's mother from the actual Ashmere cast, for this opening
conversation at Ferry Landing. This is a PM content choice, not an owner selection of
the character or approval of a Q1 quest design. Old Bram and the retired sampler stay
outside active play under the [real cast decision](owner-decision-real-chapter-cast-2026-10-05.md).

Elspeth remains fixed and reachable at every hour, applying the
[no-required-wait rule](owner-decision-no-wait-opening-2026-10-05.md) and superseding her
Green/cottage schedule in [archived chapter content](../archive/spec/00a-chapter-one-content.md#4-npcs)
for the current opening. She introduces Wren and offers directions north through Well Lane
to Village Green, or east from Well Lane to Maud at the Drowned Lantern. This dialogue
creates no Q1 acceptance, journal entry, fact or reward; Q1 and the main search remain
unimplemented. The existing cartridge NPC/dialogue and Book machinery own the interaction.
The active behavior is specified in [Cartridges](../system/cartridge.md#source-layout).

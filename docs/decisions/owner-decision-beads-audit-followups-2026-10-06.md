# Owner decision: track concrete audit follow-ups in Beads

The owner authorized adding concrete audit follow-up tasks and PRs to the Beads
Rust tracker alongside the 33 Chapter 1 completion-plan slices.

Create a supplemental task only for a concrete, actionable follow-up from a
documented audit finding. Link it to the finding and its evidence, and use
dependencies only when the work actually depends on another tracked task. The
33 planned slices remain required exactly once; supplemental follow-ups do not
replace them or change roadmap status and completion counts. The PM remains the
single Beads writer, and closure still requires the normal reviewed merge.

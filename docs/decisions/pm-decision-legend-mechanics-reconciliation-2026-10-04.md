Before live M6, prove actual lethal combat → chapel return → equipment-free, fare-free, nonhostile walk through `chapel_nave → chapel_steps → north_gate → village_green → well_lane → drowned_lantern → lantern_cellar` → recovery of actual worn/nested corpse contents. The five rats engage only on deliberate Attack. Topology alone is insufficient: the installed carrying/recovery admission must leave these possessions recoverable, including forced overload. Repeated deaths preserve each existing corpse identity.

Every later barrier consumer rechecks this guarantee before merging its unsafe content. Prefer an authored route/free return/usable light where it covers the whole journey; otherwise the consuming brief may select the single shrine action in the table. No permanent item loss is an acceptable route substitute. Optional S2 late/never remains intentional while Q2/Q3 completion stays reachable.

Mandatory rescue/bell/dream/dawn reading uses safe contexts, closable/resumable controls and actual speaker presence while world time continues. A departing Vesper cannot remain a fictitious bound speaker: interrupt and permit durable re-entry, or explicitly author the needed scene profile. Required S2 acceptance/delivery actions remain accessible and unrelated mandatory scenes are deferrable through its timed window. Ordinary combat menus retain threat/Stand/Flee; safe reading does not grant general combat immunity or extend deadlines.

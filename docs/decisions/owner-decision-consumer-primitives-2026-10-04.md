# Owner clarification: missing primitives at real consumers — 2026-10-04

Owner instruction (paraphrased): create or extend a needed reusable primitive when an
actual mechanic requires semantics the existing primitives do not express. Reuse is a
starting point, not a reason to weaken that mechanic.

This records the explicit clarification relayed in the preserved composition-audit
handoff; it is not a verbatim quotation. It extends the [emergence direction](../archive/decisions/owner-decision-emergence-2026-09-25.md)
and stays within the [autonomous mechanics delegation](owner-decision-autonomous-mechanics-2026-10-03.md).
The canonical brief/review rule is [Building mechanics by composition](../system/architecture.md#building-mechanics-by-composition).

Permission is bounded to the actual consumer and smallest missing semantic contract.
It does not require a new package, framework, general setter or full catalog implementation.
Existing documented hooks and reserved engine facts retain their contracts. New operations,
schemas and invariants follow spec-first implementation, applicable two-kernel obligations,
focused behavior proof, normal checks and fresh independent review. Cartridge numbers,
pure proposals, changed-row transactions and host/presenter boundaries remain in force.

The [dated audit evidence](../evidence/2026-10-04-primitive-composition/README.md) informs future
briefs; its static findings are not runtime reproductions or implemented fixes. This
docs change authorizes no production-source refactor and certifies no queued mechanic.

The PM amends the archived [00a §7 S3 row](../archive/spec/00a-chapter-one-content.md#7-quests)
and provisional brief explicitly: all-hours Start replaces the night-only offer;
player-triggered adjacent leader steps replace timed patrol; the original passive
Tobin replaces mortal Tobin and retired Bram's alternate turn-in; real player death
fails the attempt with immediate recovery, not the entire quest. The cyclic physical
walk replaces disconnected historical patrol jumps. Every checkpoint needs a new
causally accepted player entry beside the leader, not four repetitions of one room.
No existing active S3 implementation or normative reward is being replaced.

Success grants only reserved player trust and resolves S3 as completed. Omit the
archived/provisional swords reward: [C1](pm-decision-c1-tobin-training-2026-10-05.md)
already owns paid first acquisition plus the exact rusty gift, and acquired membership
refuses another paid lesson. A free S3 acquisition would skip that consumer or require
another gift branch. No skill, weapon, money or item is needed for patrol. Trust is
recognition, with no new gate privilege. The required North Gate–chapel corridor stays
open at every hour; the archived 18:00 closed-gate answer is not current applicability.
The Watch Cell is vacant and unlocked, avoiding a new key-grant/recovery prerequisite.

C2 moves the original C1 Tobin spawn to the reachable Watch Post; identity, funds,
lessons and the exact sword remain his. While waiting between steps he still teaches.
The typed leader patrol is independent of Wren's following escort. Four new public
watch rooms complete this row's existing room budget; no hounds, mortality, arrest,
daily scheduler, general objective interpreter or new foundation arithmetic is added.
Any required new state/delta branch retains both-kernel composition obligations.

# PM decision: B5 finite Infirmary Herbs — 2026-10-05

Under [autonomous mechanics delegation](owner-decision-autonomous-mechanics-2026-10-03.md),
the PM adopts the [S9 contract](../system/mechanics.md#s9-infirmary-herbs-b5-selected-contract)
and [chapter tuning](../system/cartridge.md#b5-herb-and-bandage-stock).
The [re-pinned brief](https://github.com/lorecrafting/lokacore/blob/f8513671ea7dd84d681876b2e36850129a4b0564/docs/briefs/chapter-one/b5-infirmary-herbs-brief-2026-10-05.md)
assigns the later source slice; this record is planning, not implementation/proof.

B1/B2 are installed at chapter0.0.16/API1.14. Local base `d41ec0d2` includes
the adopted B3 plan; that shop intentionally creates nothing and has no restock.
The earlier provisional B5 assumption of a B3 item issuance seam is therefore
incorrect. Existing authored item identities and conserved transfers supply this
consumer without extending death-only entity.create or inventing provenance.

Select finite herbs and funded bandages, deterministic harvest from actual room
custody, exact multi-item exchange, immediate explicit reacceptance, and a separate
cumulative S9 contribution. Four fully funded optional exchanges demonstrate both
the uncapped gain and an additional capped reward. Cartridge values are normative
in one place; no next-day repeat, regrowth, mint, population framework or second
stock ledger is required. Historical two-day regrowth/daily repeats remain
proposals, not prerequisites. Saturated global reputation awards no contribution;
unrelated faction loss never resets contribution already awarded by S9.

Harvested/dropped/stored/corpse-held items keep their identities. An active quest
survives temporary loss of direct custody and completes after actual retrieval;
completed rewards keep ordinary storage/death behavior. Giving away finite supply
can make an optional exchange unavailable, never a required route. C5 must supply
its own immediate recovery contract if bandage consumption becomes required;
D12 may extend herbalism only at its real consumer. No renewable mechanism is
silently promised by this decision.

PM adoption tier: Codex Sol medium under the [routing](https://github.com/lorecrafting/lokacore/blob/d9592588d145e3be131f40152b5bd01aab82ad2e/docs/decisions/pm-decision-codex-model-routing-2026-10-05.md).
Future B5 source/release/hash/IDs/PR/proof pins remain null until implementation.
Independent exact-head plan review follows; source work still needs the normal
protocol/save reviews, checks and local draft-PR workflow.

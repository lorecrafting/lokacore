# Owner decision: no waiting in the opening chapter, 2026-10-05

Owner direction (paraphrased): a first-time player should not have to wait for high or low tide, a person to return, nightfall, the next day, or a clock boundary to continue a quest. Waiting can be reconsidered in later content when the player is invested. Bram's opening quest belongs to *The Missing Child* chapter. The current Bram's Lantern errand is a development sampler, not the chapter's Q1, The Ferryman's Favor.

## Content rule

- Keep the live world clock and its tested schedule primitive. Do not offer player time skips. Remove required idle waits from the first chapter's authored route and from the current sampler's Bram errand.
- Bram remains reachable at Ferry Landing for the sampler errand and Q1 offer/turn-in. Q1 completes by reaching the Green and meeting Elspeth; it does not require a clock beat or a return trip to catch Bram. Later Bram movement may be authored only with an always-available, causally clear way to complete any active obligation.
- The Q2 mire plank is always passable as the non-swim rescue route. Swimming can remain an optional risky alternative. The fox charm is discoverable without a tide window. Tide marks, water level, and day/night can color descriptions, but cannot close either required route or its recovery path.
- North Gate remains traversable for the opening search. Tobin's trust may change its response or provide a shortcut, not make the player wait for daytime. Any chapter-one quest that previously required a night or next-day trigger must instead have a player-initiated entry or be deferred until a separately reviewed beat. An incorrect wisp answer may require another action or cost, not a night of idling before retry.
- Timed *deadlines* are distinct from waiting: S2's ledger and the Q2 bell consequence may still create urgency, subject to the existing reading/accessibility and recovery gates. Rest, reading and menu use do not jump the clock.

This supersedes the archived `00a-chapter-one-content.md` tide/window, North Gate, S3/S4/S27 and initial Bram schedule clauses only where they would require waiting. It also supersedes the current sampler Bram movement chosen for M1-B2; the schedule mechanism and its frozen conformance fixture remain intact. The active [mechanics queue](../NEXT-MECHANICS.md) records the implementation route. No tide engine is required for chapter one unless an actual optional consumer later justifies it.

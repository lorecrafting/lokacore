# Owner decision: the prologue teaches every basic through quests; houses and guilds at the landing — 2026-10-23

Written 2026-10-23 in Claude Code (PM design session). The owner's words are paraphrased.

Owner, 2026-10-23 (paraphrased): aim the abilities design at the prologue aboard the Forest Ark;
everybody can learn basic spells, skills, combat and crafting, and may specialize later. Houses and
guilds wait until the landing, after the prologue, which is still an involved game. In the prologue
a skill is learned through gifts or labour, as quests or tasks: when the questline is done the
skill is learned, and it is then used for other quests. The design's recommendations were
accepted ("I like your idea"; "go for it").

## Effect

1. The prologue has no class, house or guild choice. Everyone learns the four basics (Tending,
   Making, Guarding, Words) and the first boxes of their branches
   ([abilities design §11](../game-design/abilities-design-2026-10-23.md#11-the-prologue-aboard-the-forest-ark-added-2026-10-23)).
2. A prologue box is learned by completing a short quest of gifts or labour; the teacher's final
   choice teaches it with no coin. This sets aside the coin fee per box
   ([homestead skill path](owner-decision-homestead-skill-path-2026-10-15.md), ruling 2) for the
   prologue only; coins arrive at descent stage 4 and the coin fee applies after the prologue. A
   failed craft still loses its inputs.
3. Houses (one per character) and guilds (several) open at the landing; their design is parked.
4. Skills stay on the Character page, each profession collapsed to one line that opens its tree;
   a separate Skills page splits out at the landing, when a character can hold more than two
   professions. This replaces the trigger in the
   [Skills on Character ruling](pm-decision-skills-on-character-2026-10-15.md) ("until a second
   profession exists").
5. The prologue sampler waits until the owner lays down more prologue lore (Beads
   `loka-kgd.194.7`).

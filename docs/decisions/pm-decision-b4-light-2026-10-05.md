refillable wick; use its existing exact identity and B3 commerce. This delivers
Buy→Ignite→optional well detail→Douse→Refuel without issuing replacement gear or
building another acquisition route. The well's public return stair and the owned
corpse exception select the route required by the [recovery stop gate](pm-decision-legend-mechanics-reconciliation-2026-10-04.md#reachable-death-recovery-and-reading).

route is darkened. Finite exhausted supply is honest
optional loss of light, never required waiting or a chapter/recovery softlock.

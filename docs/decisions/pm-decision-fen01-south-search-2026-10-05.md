# PM decision: first south search route — 2026-10-05

FEN-01 adds the first four real Ashmere fen rooms to the active Missing Child chapter.
The [archived chapter room table](../archive/spec/00a-chapter-one-content.md#the-fen-22)
supplies their geography; the [active cartridge specification](../system/cartridge.md#source-layout)
defines this incremental release. The owner delegated changeable in-game wording to the
PM in the [copy decision](owner-decision-copy-delegation-2026-10-04.md).

Ferry Landing opens south to Reed Path, then south to Reed Bank, west to Willow Shade,
and south to Drowned Oak, with reciprocal exits. Reed Path's fox prints and Reed Bank's
tracks are ordinary, visible inspectable details. The route is available at every hour.
The [opening no-wait rule](owner-decision-no-wait-opening-2026-10-05.md) governs this
selection; the archived tide-dependent continuation does not enter this slice.

This is a bounded geography and observation release, not Q2 or the rescue. Wren,
discovery credit, swim and tide gates, additional items, populations, and exits to rooms
outside the four-room slice await their own decisions and consumers. Use the installed
room/detail mechanics under the [composition rule](../system/architecture.md#building-mechanics-by-composition).
Release `ashmere_missing_child@0.0.6` gets independent artifact and ID answers, and
older exact pins require explicit Start over without deleting their saves.

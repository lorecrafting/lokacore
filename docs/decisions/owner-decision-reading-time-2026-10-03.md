# Owner decision: world time continues during reading — 2026-10-03

Recorded by Codex from this session. Owner wording, verbatim:

> okay lets go with what you recommend, as long as world time does not stop

The immediately preceding PM recommendation was:

> My recommendation is **avoid punishing reading speed**: keep timed decisions intentional, and provide protection or an escape option when controls are restricted.

## Approved direction

World time keeps running during dialogue, menus and cutscenes. Reading must not introduce a
world-clock pause or a player-driven time skip. Design these interactions so that reading speed
does not become an unintended test: timed choices are intentional, and restricted controls need
protection or a usable way to respond to danger.

This is direction for the later time-model and story integration, not a live clock or protection
mechanic installed by the current modal-scene slice. The [chapter-one plan](owner-decision-chapter-one-plan-2026-10-02.md)
continues to govern Gate C1. The existing time-rate target remains in the
[untimed-Lantern decision](owner-decision-untimed-lantern-2026-10-02.md).

## PM planning interpretation, not additional owner wording

Use safe contexts for mandatory modal scenes. Menus and dialogue must be interruptible when
danger requires a response; an interface must not remove escape controls while harmful events
continue to affect the player. Ordinary inspection should not confer a general combat immunity.
Story deadlines and irreversible outcomes need explicit authored treatment of required reading
and clear presentation of intentional timed choices.

The future slice must settle start eligibility, interruption, affected timers and due-event
ordering together, amending `docs/system` and applicable contracts before implementation.
Specific protection mechanics, combat/death rules, fully terminated-app catch-up and persistence
are not selected here. Any such design must respect the requirement that world time does not stop;
this does not promise continuous OS execution or approve additional mechanics proposals.

Effect: the [owner rules](../system/owner-rules.md#product-and-scope) and future time/story briefs.
Installed command timing, the modal scene contract, the parked touch UI and the sampler's pending
identity/prose approvals are unchanged.

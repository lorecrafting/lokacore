Agent-written, so reference or placeholder under the [human-made content rule](../decisions/owner-decision-human-made-content-2026-10-31.md): none of it ships as written, and the owner writes shipped prose. | Where | Prose it holds |
|---|---|
| `cartridges/toolbox_showcase/text.json` | every text key: actions, narration, room and item words |
| `cartridges/toolbox_showcase/{rooms,items,npcs,dialogues,scenes,quests,topics,statuses,reactions}/` | names, descriptions, speech and choices |
| `cartridges/toolbox_showcase/transcripts/` | recorded play of that text |
| `cartridges/lantern_proof/` (same layout) | the proof chapter's text |
| `cartridges/*_sampler/` | each sampler's text, as a test fixture |

The [showcase plans](../SHOWCASE.md) and the [Chapter 1 briefs](../briefs/chapter-one/README.md) carry their own marks. Where the owner rewrites a file's text, drop its row.

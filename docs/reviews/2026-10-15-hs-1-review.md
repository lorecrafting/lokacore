Probe: a rule `item_dropped -> replenish.run sweep` moves `ore_01` mine->store. Take `ore_01`, then Drop it: the Drop faults `conflicting_write` and nothing commits. The hs-1 Known limits (`:2689`) do not. hs-4's feed (Put `grain_01` in the trough, then `item_acquired -> replenish.run spent_grain`) does the same thing, so every feed Put would fault. The Step bullet (`:2686`) says "the group an earlier write of the same advance gave the item", which reads as covering the root command's write; hs-4's feed was designed on that reading.

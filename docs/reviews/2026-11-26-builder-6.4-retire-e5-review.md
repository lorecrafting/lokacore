## Findings

1. **Blocker**, `mobile/app/author/build/page.ts:18`; the test is at `page.test.ts:19,64`.
   - The test's event targets are plain objects, never `HTMLElement`, so `typing` is always false in the suite.
   - Mutant `t instanceof HTMLElement || textField(t)` stays green. In a browser every target is an `HTMLElement`, so under it every double-click opens nothing and every walk key is ignored.

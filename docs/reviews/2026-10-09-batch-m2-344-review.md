## Findings

1. **Blocker.** `kernel/ts/src/mechanics/combat/credit.ts:49` writes the kill's `levelling.set` in
   the fatal writer group; `kernel/ts/src/mechanics/reaction.ts:126` writes the reaction's grant in
   the reaction's own group. Both are spec'd experience sources (row 4), and the composer faults any
   same-target write from two groups (`kernel/ts/src/foundation/compose.ts:67-70`). Scenario: a
   cartridge lists an NPC in `kills` and rewards its kill quest (objective on the `death_credit`
   fact, as `ashmere_sampler` does) with a `quest_resolved` reaction `experience.grant`. The elapsed
   step that kills the NPC faults `conflicting_write` on `{kind: levelling}`; the round can never be
   delivered, so play stalls. Probe: `levelling_sampler` copy with `death_credit` on `rat_a`, the
   quest objective on that fact and the reward reaction on it; the killing `stepElapsed` returns
   `{"kind":"fault","code":"conflicting_write","target":{"kind":"levelling",...}}`. Control with
   `rat_a` dropped from `kills`: accepted, one `levelling.set` (group 3, expected null → 20).
   Expected fix: one experience write per character per proposal (the reaction already sums its
   grants into `gained`; do the same across the proposal, e.g. where `propose()` already derives
   `levelUp`), or another spec'd arrangement; a test with both sources in one step; row 4's "several
   kills in one decision chain their writes" reworded to what the composer admits.
2. **Should-fix.** Row 4 text (`docs/system/mechanics.md`, Experience bullet) promises chained
   writes for several kills in one decision, but two deaths credited in one step would sit in two
   fatal groups and fault as in finding 1. Fix with finding 1.

## Findings

1. **blocker**, `kernel/ts/src/mechanics/containment/react.ts:87` (and the Elixir loader `lib/loka/content/reactions.ex` consequence check): `npc.move` jumps an NPC that is some character's following escort. Then `escort/shared.ts:106-119` `following()` throws `precondition_failed` on every player Move and Flee (`travel()`), a navigation soft-lock until death separates them. escort@1 forbids teleport ("never detachment or teleport"). Reachable by content: `npc.move {npc: <escort npc>, room}` on any trigger, or subject-based (`attack_result` target, `status_applied`, `fact_changed` on the escort). A loader refusal like `content/cartridge_patrol.ts:95` covers only the named form; the subject form needs a runtime no-op when the NPC is any character's active following escort. Add a red control. The spec's "Missing invariant: none" should then name it.
2. **should-fix**, `react.ts:87`: no `moves()` guard. A rooted or paralysed NPC is jumped, while every other NPC mover honors it (`schedule/rule.ts:135` `still = from === room || !moves(world, npc)`; crow, flight, population, patrol). The schema and spec say "as a schedule move does", and the spec's Known limits contradicts that. Disposition: add `|| !moves(world, npc)`, or drop "as a schedule move does" from schema and spec.
3. **should-fix**, `mechanics/reaction.ts:84-87` with `runtime/proposal.ts:235` `triggered(p.world, ...)`: `witness` is the first filter that reads mutable state (containers, life, blindness, concealment), but it reads the pre-command world, while `npc.move`'s `living`/`engaged` in the same slice read the composed world. Scenarios: `entity_died` with `witness` = the victim holds although the schema says "alive"; in one long `wait`, a schedule job moves the baker out at 08:00 and a 10:00 `attack_result` or bleed death still counts him. Disposition: evaluate `witness` on the composed world, or record a Known limit and correct "alive" in schema and spec.
4. nit: no PR description, so no `/code-review` result is reported (batch model); report it at the batch PR.
5. nit: `kernel/ts/test/longwait_property.test.ts` is absent from the base (#384 not merged into `toolbox/batch-m13`); the FD5 sampler rules are not yet exercised by it. Rerun after the batch sync.

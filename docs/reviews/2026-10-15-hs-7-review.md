## Findings

1. **should-fix** `docs/system/mechanics.md:3520`: the known limit says "the farmer still gains trust" when another NPC receives the apple. The probe shows the opposite. The holder (the trader) gains trust, the farmer gains nothing, and the once-only flag is read at the farmer, so a non-farmer recipient is paid again on every regive. In this sampler, giving the apple to the trader is a legal move and the farmer's warm tier then becomes unreachable (there is no way to get the apple back). There is no faucet here: trust is bounded and nothing returns the apple. Fix: state the real behavior.
2. **nit** `docs/system/mechanics.md:1579`: the 20b limit gives the reason as "the service price minimum is unchanged ... a protocol change". hs-7 made that protocol change. Claim and cancel stay at least 1 because of the loader `.price` check. A reader would look for the wrong guard.
3. **nit** process: the developer's `/code-review` ran in the wrong worktree (loka-0w4), so no result is reported for this diff. Run it in the fix round.
4. **nit** `mobile/authority/local-story/gift.test.ts`: `gift_sampler` has no shop, so `commerceSave` never sees a price-0 `use_service` receipt. If someone changes `commerce-save.ts:51` to always expect a payment pair, a shop cartridge with a barter would fail to reopen, and no test would catch it.

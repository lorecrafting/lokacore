## Harness limit

On iOS, safaridriver raises no software keyboard and inserts no text. This was checked on a bare
`<input>` on `about:blank`: `keydown` fires, but there is no `input` event, and `visualViewport` stays the
same height. Text was therefore injected (native value setter plus an `input` event), and Enter was
sent as a key action. **Keyboard coverage could not be measured**, so it goes on the owner's list.
The app has no `visualViewport` or keyboard handling anywhere (grep of `mobile/app`).

## Safe areas

can sit under the notch or home indicator. `html` and `body` backgrounds are transparent. The white bands
in the screenshots are the status-bar and toolbar areas outside the web view, not a gap in the page.

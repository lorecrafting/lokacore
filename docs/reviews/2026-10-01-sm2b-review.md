## Findings

- **N1 nit** `mobile/app/book/model.test.ts:45`: one link in the literal, so a greedy `\(.*\)` in `plain` passes; a description with two touch links would then lose the prose between them. Add a second link to the literal.
- **N2 nit** `mobile/app/book/pages.tsx:82`: Bram's page (an NPC with no actions) reads "There is nothing to do with it." Neutral wording ("Nothing to do here yet.") avoids calling a person "it".
- **N3 nit** `mobile/app/book/Book.tsx:36`: the log start is keyed to the button tapped, not the action the controller sends. While a `take` is pending, tapping north retries the take but resets the log start; tapping scan while a move is pending retries the move without resetting it. Only the log window shifts, nothing is lost from the save; acceptable for the prototype, worth knowing for SM2c.
- **N4 nit** `mobile/app/book/pages.tsx:14`: `fontStyle: 'italic'` with no italic face renders upright on iOS (screenshot) and may be synthesized as a slant on Android; drop it so both platforms match.

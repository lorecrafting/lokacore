## 4. Findings

- **B1 (blocker), `kernel/ts/src/actions.ts:216`.**
  - The speaker guard in `accepts` is untested.
  - Failure scenario, run on the mutant: the ferry with a second NPC `ada` in the landing whose dialogue `ada` has policy `quest_state lantern = resolved`. With the quest active, the command `talk {target_id: ADA}` returns:
    - with the guard: rejected `invalid_state`;
    - without it: accepted `choice_opened` of ada's dialogue. `refusal` matches bram's available talk, and the rule then finds ada's dialogue by target, so ada's policy is bypassed.
  - The code-review fix (0726ddd) tests `lists()` only.
  - Fix: one test of that scenario.
- **B2 (blocker), `kernel/ts/src/dialogue.ts:103`.**
  - Nothing checks that the dialogue's policy gates its talk.
  - With `policy: ALWAYS`, both suites stay green.
  - Failure scenario: a talk before `accept` or after the quest resolves opens a choice. Decision 9 says talk is unavailable then.
  - Fix: one test asserting that talk is refused before accept and after resolve (admission's code).
- **N1 (nit), `kernel/ts/src/proposal.ts:215`.**
  - The docstring says quest deliveries count toward `deliveries`, but no test exercises that.
  - Scenario: an event earning more instances than the composition-profile limit is accepted, when it should be `budget_exceeded`. That needs a contrived cartridge.

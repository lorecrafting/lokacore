The round's group is held under the body key, `statusGroup` (`status/job.ts:247`) joins the tick to it, and the refill looks only at the pool key, so it writes its own group; the tick's hp row then conflicts.

Reproduced through `ClockDriver` on real SQLite, no forged state: the sampler plus one status (`tick_every` 150, `per_tick` -1 on hp, applied by a rule on entering `pit`). Move east, kill rats a, b, c, pulse 150 each. Invocation ids `dddddddd-8888-4888-8888-<n>` and `ffffffff-...`: job order `npc@450,status@450`.

- Third pulse: `fault conflicting_write`, clock stays 300, hp 8, level 1.
- Pulse retried, 3600 more wall time: same fault. `look` and `flee`: delivery fault too, since each drains first. After a reopen: same. Reopen itself succeeds, so it is a permanent soft lock, not a refusal.
- 2 of 8 id prefixes hit this order; 6 of 8 took another order, level 2, hp 10.

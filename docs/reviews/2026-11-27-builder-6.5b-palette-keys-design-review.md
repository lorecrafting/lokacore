## Findings

1. **should-fix** `mobile/app/author/build/build.ts:48` with `palette.tsx:63,147`: with four sheets open Cmd-K replaces the top sheet; Delete then targets the sheet under it, so the author deletes a definition they were not looking at (Esc also loses the replaced sheet). The delete is one Undo-chip step (`roomedit.tsx:87` `run`), so it can be put back; otherwise this would be a blocker. Fix: the palette drops the lowest sheet when the stack is full, never the top (spec written).
2. **should-fix** `palette.tsx:126`: no Up/Down and no marked row; Enter runs a row nothing marks, and the panel (`tools/author-panel/src/palette.tsx:33-36`), which retires at 6.8, has both. Ruling: required. Fix: an active row index, Up/Down move it (reset on query change), the row drawn with `act`'s `selected` fill (plus `aria-selected`), Enter runs it.
3. **should-fix** `palette.tsx:131-138`: rows sit flush to the sheet edge while the header and query sit at `space.md` (pal390-dark, pal1280-dark). Fix: wrap the rows in a `View` with `paddingHorizontal: space.md`.
4. **should-fix** `palette.tsx:133` with `kernel/ts/play/palette.ts:47`: rows still to write read `~ <key> · <file>`; Build never shows `~` (ruling 9; Checks reads `<key>: unwritten`). Fix: the Builder labels a `todo` row `<key>: unwritten · <file>`. Typing `~` still filters.
5. **should-fix** `map.tsx:197-201`: keys run only when the ground itself has focus (`e.target === e.currentTarget`); a click on a room focuses that room's button, so arrows, `+` `-` `0` and PageUp/PageDown then do nothing. Fix: take every key from inside the ground except Enter and Space on a room button.
6. **nit** `palette.tsx:66` and `69,79`: Delete with nothing deletable, and Undo after the chip has gone, do nothing visible. Ruling: such a row is not listed (spec written).
7. **nit** `map.tsx:159` with `palette.tsx:125`: the focus ring is clipped to its top and bottom edges. In mapkeys.png the `action` lines sit above and below the ground with none at its sides, and in pal390 the query field shows a line only beneath it. The sheet's scroll view clips the outline. Fix: inset the focused element by `space.xs` (at least `2 × size.focus`) from the scroll view's edges, or clip with padding.
8. **question** `mapcontrols.tsx:70`: an arrow can select a room outside the shown ground at high zoom. The bar names it, but should the view pan to it? Not ruled here.

Focus: the query field's `autoFocus` comes after the title's focus ref, and the screenshots show its ring, so the field takes focus (now in the spec). Close follows `back()`'s stack rule. Touch at 390: rows are `act` (`size.touch` tall, full width), and the query is taller than `size.touch`.

Owner word items (placeholders, marked in code): `Commands`, `Type a command, a key or words`, the COMMANDS labels (`New actions`, plurals as kind names), `unwritten`.

Proposed check: a `opened()` unit test, with four sheets open plus the palette, that the sheet under the palette is the one it was opened over.

Not covered: anything live, true dark Book paper, and a screen reader.

Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED (should-fix 1-5).

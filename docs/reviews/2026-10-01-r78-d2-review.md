## Requirements derived from the spec before reading the diff

From 23 §§3-5, 03 §26 and the brief's settlement:

1. A cartridge declares story points (key, outcomes), each outcome reached by one rule-owned
   trigger (a dialogue and its choice). The declaration is closed. It is an optional
   `story_points` map keyed by DefinitionRefString of kind `story_point`.
2. The compiler and the loader agree, with literal paths and codes, on: an unresolved dialogue,
   a choice the dialogue lacks, a site feeding two outcomes, a trigger dialogue with no quest,
   no outcome, and the owner (dialogue@1) not locked. No new diagnostic code.
3. `choose` emits exactly one typed `story_point_reached {story_point, outcome}` as its last
   event, with the **declared outcome key**. It never fires on talk, close_choice, a refusal or
   a fault. The earlier rule-minted ids stay put.
4. The host turns that event into a pending report committed in the same transaction as the
   decision. A receipt replay and a reopen-replay add none. The validate guard stays.
   `Host.story_points` and the `custom_event` mapping are gone.
5. Every known answer and hash stays byte-identical except the ferry's. No recipe trigger and no
   test-only production code.

## Findings

**F1 (blocker): the outcome mutant survives every suite.** At kernel/ts/src/dialogue.ts:58,
replace `outcome: outcome as Key` with `outcome: choice_id`. kernel/ts, local-story and mix
suites all stay green. The reason: every ferry outcome is named after its choice (`carry→carry`,
`leave→leave`, cartridges/ashmere_ferry/story_points/lantern_resolved.json), and so is the
"own dialogue" test (kernel/ts/test/dialogue.test.ts:344-350). Failure scenario: a cartridge
declares `abandoned: {dialogue: bram, choice: leave}`. The mutant emits and reports
`outcome: 'leave'`, which the platform rejects as an invalid outcome (23 §5), or it is recorded
under the wrong outcome. Fix: one test whose outcome key differs from its choice id, with a
hand-written expected payload.

**F2 (should-fix): "last" is not pinned on a choice that changes no fact.** At
kernel/ts/src/rules/dialogue.ts:119, swap to `...reached, resolvedChoice`. The ferry tests stay
green, because fact.ts `factChanged` re-sorts by position only when a fact changes. A probe
(ferry, leave with its `sequence` removed) gave final positions `[3, story_point_reached],
[4, choice_resolved]` under the mutant, against `[3, choice_resolved], [4, story_point_reached]`
at head. Failure scenario: a story-point choice with no fact.assign emits the story point before
`choice_resolved`, against Decision 8 and event.schema's "the last event". Fix: the F1 test can
use a choice with no sequence and assert the positions too.

**F3 (should-fix): a helper with one caller.** `reachedBy` (kernel/ts/src/dialogue.ts:47-60) is
exported from the shared module, yet its only caller is `choose` (rules/dialogue.ts:109). The
brief's acceptance says "no helper with one caller". rules/dialogue.ts is 145 lines and has no
size allowance problem, so the scan can be inlined in `choose` and the export dropped.

**N1 (nit): an unwrapped doc line.** mobile/authority/local-story/authority.ts:185 is a
134-column line left unwrapped in the rewritten doc comment.

  one-shot per quest instance. The binding test still discriminates across two runs: reached
  under B, then null, it reports A, then C.

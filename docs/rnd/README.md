# R&D

Exploratory studies for future product directions. Informative only: nothing here is a spec, a decision or a commitment.

- [AI dungeon master and living world (2026-10-11)](ai-world-shaping-2026-10-11.md): can an AI build and reshape the world on the deterministic kernel; guardrail rings, dials versus rules, staged experiments (Beads loka-e97d).

- [Settle: settlement and defense (2026-10-14)](settle-design-2026-10-14.md): Fable design plan for S3/S4 (loka-mytp, loka-oxgk): structures attract named residents, upkeep by wage job, full-moon raids held or sacked by the watch; two new engine pieces (narrate step, npc.move by plan); five slices; owner questions answered ([decision](../decisions/owner-decision-settle-and-fd7-2026-10-14.md)).
- [Player-built rooms and houses (2026-10-11)](built-rooms-2026-10-11.md): blueprints in the cartridge, built instances in the save; layered exits; build, decay, repair, raze; a built-rooms layer after M11 (Beads loka-kgd.109).
- [Cloudflare services for Loka (2026-10-11)](cloudflare-2026-10-11.md): what Cloudflare is, a plain catalogue of its services, which ones fit Loka and why, what to avoid, an adoption path with costs, and a reading list (Beads loka-2of for heavy checks).
- [Depth, immersion and touch UX (2026-10-13)](depth-and-ux-2026-10-13.md): ranked mechanics gaps, content tooling, Realm-readiness risks and touch UX ideas; adopted by the owner, filed in Beads (loka-kgd.138-148, loka-kgd.107.20-25, loka-x6t.39-52).
- [Realm seams audit (2026-10-13)](realm-seams-audit-2026-10-13.md): read-only audit of the kernel for single-player assumptions (`world.character` reads and similar) against the Realm seams owner decision (Beads loka-kgd.150).
- [Realm live operations (2026-10-29)](realm-live-ops-2026-10-29.md): proposal for shipping content and mechanics to a live Realm without a restart (artifact swap, shadow replay, canary, versioned mechanics, rollback); Beads loka-cw9y.
- [Raph Koster design lessons (2026-10-15)](koster-design-lessons-2026-10-15.md): LegendMUD, Ultima Online and Star Wars Galaxies lessons for professions, hidden elites, crafting interdependence, economy, housing and the Realm, with a checklist for Loka; research notes on [LegendMUD and UO](koster-research-notes/legendmud-ultima-online.md), [SWG](koster-research-notes/star-wars-galaxies.md), [later talks](koster-research-notes/talks-later-career.md) and [writings](koster-research-notes/writings-principles.md) (Beads loka-ikch).
- [Morrowind's character system (2026-10-23)](elder-scrolls-morrowind-skills-2026-10-23.md): skills, levelling, trainers, magic and factions in Morrowind, with Skyrim and SWG contrasts and lessons for Loka's open basics (Beads loka-kgd.194).
- [Design audit: abilities and authoring (2026-10-23)](design-audit-abilities-authoring-2026-10-23.md): Fable's audit of the abilities design, the authoring-language and inheritance proposals; the phased foundation plan (Beads loka-kgd.194).
- [World of Warcraft and "WoW Forever" design lessons (2026-11-03)](wow-and-classic-forever-design-lessons-2026-11-03.md): quest hub clustering, horizontal mastery over power creep, danger gradients, rested haven framing, world-anchored crafting and macro-landmark spatial orientation for Book UI cartridges.
- [Legendary RPG and MUD design breakthroughs (2026-11-03)](rpg-and-mud-design-breakthroughs-2026-11-03.md): affliction-based triage (Achaea), handcrafted quests granting world verbs (OSRS), internal voices and failing forward (Disco Elysium), looping shortcuts and item lore (Dark Souls), adversarial mentors (Planescape/KOTOR II), and unprompted virtue tracking (Ultima IV).
- [Prologue adoption shortlist (2026-11-04)](prologue-adoption-shortlist-2026-11-04.md): one ranked list from the three studies below, each row mapped to Loka systems with engine cost and writing load.
- [Baldur's Gate 3 lessons (2026-11-04)](baldurs-gate-3-lessons-2026-11-04.md): reactivity, "say yes" design, visible checks and failing forward, companions, the narrator, the Nautiloid as onboarding, and what not to copy.
- [RPG genre innovations (2026-11-04)](rpg-genre-innovations-2026-11-04.md): Fallout/New Vegas, Witcher 3, Deus Ex, Ultima VII, Failbetter and storylets, Outer Wilds, Wildermyth, Hades, Roadwarden and others; techniques that multiply a solo writer's words.
- [Adoption map of the earlier notes (2026-11-04)](rnd-adoption-map-2026-11-04.md): every lesson from the Koster, Morrowind, abilities-audit, WoW and RPG/MUD notes as one row: Loka system, built or not, engine cost, writing load, prologue fit.
- [Cartridge source by world area: design (2026-11-04)](cartridge-area-layout-design-2026-11-04.md): Fable's proposal for area folders, border rules, lint checks, a generated area index and the showcase migration; owner rulings pending (Beads loka-vduxr.58).
- [GUI editor: every mechanic with create, edit and delete (2026-11-04)](gui-editor-design-2026-11-04.md): Fable's design for the authoring tool: Book contextual actions, Map edits and one Edit tab of mechanic blocks; the watcher's write path with `was` checks and undo; every capability mapped to a surface; a CRUD coverage gate; seven lean slices; owner rulings pending (Beads loka-wfrgk.1).
- [Story map: a generated narrative view (2026-11-05)](story-map-design-2026-11-05.md): Fable's design for the editor's Story view, generated from the compiled artifact over `reach.ts`'s content graph: nodes and edges by artifact field, four views (Flow, List, Character, Trace), an `outline.json` beats overlay with seeds and payoffs, where each check runs, play from a node, budgets, seven slices; owner questions pending (Beads loka-wfrgk.18).
- [NPC behaviours: NPCs that act as players do (2026-11-06)](npc-behaviours-2026-11-06.md): Fable's second opinion on the owner's NPC behaviour decisions and the plan: verb helpers run for an NPC body from one behaviour job (no NPC CharacterId), a four-layer arbiter with one-level resume, plans as a fixed step vocabulary, canned behaviours as copied templates, hour-mark catch-up, the per-verb opening audit, sixteen slices with the minimum-alive set, the immersion catalogue as appendix; open questions for the owner.

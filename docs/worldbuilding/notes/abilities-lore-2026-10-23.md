# Abilities: lore proposals (draft, 2026-10-23)

Not canon, and not the owner's words: proposals from the design session, for the owner to
accept, change or drop. The mechanics are in
[abilities design](../../game-design/abilities-design-2026-10-23.md). Names are placeholders.

## Professions in the world

- A profession is a **way of tending**: the work the second wave does to hold the sanctuary
  ([premise](../00-foundations/premise-and-pillars.md), pillar 3). Tiller, Wright, Healer, Scout,
  Guard and Scholar are the six tendings the first settlers needed.
- A trainer's fee is a **gift to the teaching house**, which feeds teachers who no longer work
  the fields. A novice lesson is free because every child is owed the first lesson.
- Titles come from mastery, deeds or office
  ([titles](../15-professions/titles-and-recognition.md)). A master title is spoken by the
  teaching house, so only its region knows it at first.

## Two kinds of power

Proposed from [source and access](../02-magic/source-and-access.md) and
[the Power](../01-cosmology/the-power.md).

| | Coarse arts | Resonance |
|---|---|---|
| Source | the divided elements of heavy matter: fire, water, earth, lightning, force | the Primordial Resonance |
| Who | anyone, by effort and rote | a mind climbing the gradual path |
| Feel | heat, strain, ash on the tongue | stillness, the pulse of the World Tree in the chest |
| What it takes | breath, ash and herbs, weariness | stillness, gathered by sitting quiet at dawn places |
| Shadow | each harmful working leaves **weight** (karmic sediment) | weight dims it; a heavy mind cannot hear it |
| Words | common words of command | the sacred Name and its syllables (the Sacred Remembrance) |

- **Weight** is the one bridge between them. Harmful coarse workings and cruel deeds add it;
  giving, sparing, and keeping vows lift it. The world shows weight as it shows everything:
  through the land and through what people sense in you, never as a lecture
  ([pillar 4](../00-foundations/premise-and-pillars.md)).
- First contact with resonance is a moment of selfless stillness (source and access, point 3).
  In play: sparing a hurt beast, sharing the last water, sitting at the High Terrace at dawn.
  These are the warden path's stirring stage.

## The warden order as a hidden profession

- Its path is the gradual path of [warden path](../03-orders/warden-path-and-unlock.md):
  foundational scope (stirring), middle scope (search and trial: the three masteries), great
  scope (acceptance and the vow).
- Its routes in begin in tending, not in war: from healing (calm), scholarship (lore) or
  guarding (watch), always with conduct.
- Its powers are sensing, calming, cleansing weight, and the Name. It cannot raise the dead,
  conquer, or command minds
  ([limits](../02-magic/limits-and-impossibilities.md), to be written).
- Its vows bind the hand: a warden under vow does not strike the fleeing or the unarmed. A
  broken vow is a fall; a fallen warden is a path of its own (open).

## The prologue: learning is the descent

- In the luminous state no one needs a word or a tool. Each basic the player learns aboard the
  ark is a step of the deliberate descent
  ([main arc, Act I](../21-story/main-storyline-arc.md)); the first light word is learned the
  night the inner light goes out.
- Aboard the ark nothing is bought: a teacher asks for a gift or a task, and the lesson is the
  answer to work well done. Coins come with the fourth stage of the descent.
- Aboard the ark there are teachers, not houses. Households and guilds belong to the heavy world
  after the landing (owner 2026-10-23, paraphrased: deferred until the landing).

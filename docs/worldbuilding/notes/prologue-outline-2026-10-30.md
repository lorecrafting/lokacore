# Prologue outline (working draft, 2026-10-30)

Not canon. Written by Fable from the v2 Seedship Forest design (lorecrafting/lokacore-v2-legacy, docs/game-design/seedship-forest-world/) and the 2026-10-16 worldbuilding drafts, under the owner's rulings of 2026-10-30 recorded in Beads loka-g5hh. Next layers: a one-page prologue pitch, a player-verb pass per beat, the map, then scene sheets for Part 1.


A free, complete short story of 3 to 5 hours, ending on the landing. Rebuilt under the owner's ruling of 2026-10-30: the story illustrates the three capacities (wanting happiness within the turning; wanting out of the turning; coming back down to help others out) through the Three Roots, in the world's own words only. Binding: the preserved sleeper; the three-step opening ending with "The sleeper has awakened"; no economy; mechanics unlock with the descent; no borrowed franchise names.

Narration: second person, present tense; the opening dream (beat 2) is the one first-person passage. 48 beats, all main, five parts of about 45 minutes each (Part 3 under an hour).

---

## 1. Merge decisions

**The descent is the Third Root enacted by a whole ship (changed; rites accepted, owner 2026-10-30).** v2 and my earlier drafts had the fall caused by craving or caught by kindness, which fought the teaching. Now: the Grove's people are light-born beings who agreed, before the voyage, to come down into heavy bodies so that a dead world could be seeded for the ones after. The way down is a set of old rites "for when the sleeper wakes": the first fruit, the first night, the first fire, the first cloth, the first cut, the first blood. Every Tender performs them as custom whose meaning is lost; the Rememberers know each rite is a stair. The revelation is Maren's: "We did not fall. We came down. Every rite was a stair." The coarsening is chosen, step by step, by the community; the player sees it as a world darkening and fears the Blight.

**Craving's fall has one face: Dov (new; accepted, owner 2026-10-30).** So that the teaching is shown and not told, one Gatherer falls the old way: he loves the fruit most, hides it under his bedding ("Mine. For the cold," the first "mine" in the Grove), grows heavy and angry, blames the sleeper, and strikes Thera. The blow is brief and not graphic. Maren at the end: "Dov fell. The rest of us came down. The difference was only in the choosing."

**The Second Root has a living, honourable face: Seren (changed; accepted, owner 2026-10-30).** In v2 she was dead thirty years. Now she lives in the Deep caves, luminous and almost gone, going out slowly like a lamp. She was the chosen Heart; at the root she saw the turning clearly (every seeded world grows walls and burns) and chose to go out rather than plant one more field for the fire. She speaks once, with full dignity, and no one in the story calls her wrong. Brennan brings her water she no longer drinks. Her lamp goes out, without smoke, as the ship phases in.

**Thera's yearning comes from seeing, not burnout (changed).** Her exhaustion is still there, but what makes her want to stop is the Three Roots stone, Seren's words, and the Rootsong showing her the Hearth burning: she grasps that the fall repeats every age. "We come down so it can fall again?"

**The offer of rest is truly good (kept, sharpened).** Lira at the Heartroot: "Seren's way is a true way. The thirst ends and nothing is lost that was ever yours. I will not call you back from it. Rest is not false. It is only alone." Remaining is greater only because of love.

**Both halves land in the climax.** Wisdom: "When you look for yourself, what do you find?" "I can't find her." Compassion: "The love is still here. It can't rest while they're lost."

**The bond is implicit; no romance (owner 2026-10-30).** Nothing between Thera and the player is named. The love is the kind that cannot rest while others are lost, shown in what she does (the first meal at your hearth, binding your wound, teaching you her work so the sick are tended after her) and what you share (the Small Grove, the vigil). No "I love you", no confession, no dream of "someone who stays". Her dream is a morning no one needs her and a field tended for the field's sake.

**The player is the witness who sees all three lived.** The file says "witness-class"; Lira says "who tells the tale?" Four quiet choices track the player's leaning for the paid books; nothing is scored or shown: the hidden fruit (15), the answer to Seren (24), hold or strike Dov (42), keep vigil with her or let her go to the root alone (43).

**The sacrifice.** Thera is the one Heart, the last stair, the person who goes all the way down into the stone so the Tree can root. The Vowed, a circle of dozens with Tomas named first, sit in the root-alcoves and spark the rooting. (PM default, owner may change)

**Cut from my v2 draft, and why.** Yara's journal (Thera's mother is simply dead; the pendant is "my mother's"); Oda (Kira now makes blankets, fire, blades and the hut, so the First Root has one maker's face); Pell and Hesk; the humming stone; the Quiet; the Nine Sectors; the Blight healing loop. Each either repeated a capacity another character already carries or needed hours the free story does not have.

**Unchanged.** Seer = Maren on the High Terrace. No economy: tools are wood and stone, the trowel is the only metal thing, nothing is bought. Magic weakens with the descent. The opening's three steps; Tomas says the line because Lira woke the sleeper and Lira speaks through Tomas, and because the words are the first line of a rite everyone knows and no one understands. The other cradles stay sealed. (PM default, owner may change)

## 2. The beats

### Part 1: The Radiant Grove (beats 1 to 9, about 45 min)

Mechanics: movement, looking, dialogue, gifts and trust, one quest, lore, effortless resonance. Ends with hunger.

1. The Chronicler: "In the beginning of this great eon..." and the praises of those who refuse the quiet of the high places and come down into the burning house. Overtones resolve into words. — Purpose: the voice, and the Third Root sung before anyone can understand it.
2. The dream, first person: "I am light among lights. There is no I. Then there is." A hum, a lullaby; a brown sphere far below; "You're close now. Almost there." "I am falling—" — Purpose: the luminous state and the birth of "I"; seeds the lullaby and the sphere.
3. The Awakening Clearing, a perfect oval. Tomas kneels with his trowel; his eyes focus: "The sleeper has awakened." The clarity drains. It is the first line of an old rite; every child knows the words. — Purpose: the homage; the rites begin.
4. Thera: "Give them space. I'm here." The Healing Grove; her pendant swings over you: "My mother's. I never take it off." "We don't get visitors. But there is a rite for you." — Purpose: safety, the first face of care; pendant seed.
5. First steps: no weight, no print in the moss, no sun to find. The Heartwood. Maren's look held too long. Kira, hands full of woven reed: "So you're the sleeper. Then it starts. Fine. Come." — Purpose: the pristine state; the community's unease; Kira as the one who makes.
6. The Pulse, at the Spring. "Feel that rhythm? The heartbeat of the Grove." You feel it without trying. She hums; you have heard it somewhere. "I feel like I was waiting for you." — Purpose: effortless resonance; lullaby paid half-way.
7. The memorial: fresh flowers no one tends. Tomas: "She was smiling. Why won't anyone tell me why?" Dov picks fruit and gives you the best with both hands: "Not yet. Not till the rite." Thera: "Nothing here is owed. Everything is given." — Purpose: Lira; the gift rule; Dov's love of the fruit.
8. Tending with Kira in the fields: soil that never tires. The trowel turns up in a furrow: the only metal thing you have ever seen, marked in a script no one reads. Tomas: "My father brought it from... before." — Purpose: the quest loop; the Hearth's script, seed for your cradle.
9. The Rite of First Fruit. Maren: "When the sleeper wakes, those who will come down, eat." Everyone eats. You eat. Hunger, like a second heart; behind every trunk, a shadow. On the High Terrace Maren watches the twilight: "It has begun." — Purpose: the first stair, chosen; hunger unlocked.

### Part 2: The Cold Wind (beats 10 to 17, about 45 min)

Mechanics: night, sleep and dreams, thirst, cold, light and fire, weather, clothing.

10. The Rite of First Night. The sky dims to a pulse-glow; no stars. Kira hands out blankets she wove that day: "There have never been stars. Stars are a story." — Purpose: night; the no-stars seed.
11. Sleep, and a dream in second person: stars, the hum, a cold room with rows of cradles. You wake with a word you don't know. — Purpose: origin seed.
12. Thirst and cold. The Spring chills; wind through the Western Groves; leaves fall. Kira strikes the first fire. Thera: "The rites have always been. The Blight has not." — Purpose: mechanics; the mystery is the connection.
13. The Eastern Grove: trees dying in a line that turns at a right angle. Brennan from the trees, badge on his coat: "The Edges are not for you." Behind him the sky flickers. "Reflections." — Purpose: the Blight; the Watcher.
14. Fireside. Maren tells the Sweet Earth tale as a story for children: beings who tasted the crust and lost their light, who hoarded, who built the first walls. Dov laughs loudest. Thera: "You need rest." "I'm not broken." "Promise me you won't go to the Edges. Some paths are walked alone." — Purpose: the teaching as a tale; Dov's seed; her first friction.
15. Dov's hut in the Understory. Fruit hidden under his bedding. "Mine. For the cold." The first "mine" you have heard here. You carry it back to the hearth, or leave it. — Purpose: craving's fall begins in one man; a leaning choice.
16. The first rain stains; the moss holds your print. You lose the path in fog toward the Thinning. Edge sickness. "Don't move. I'm coming." Her hand on your forehead; she is pale and shaking. "That was foolish." — Purpose: she saves you, at a cost she hides.
17. The Rite of First Cloth: Kira's coat on your shoulders; night pitch-black; lanterns. Walking back from the fire: "Sometimes I wonder what it would be like to just... stop. Become soil." "Ignore me." Dream: the sphere has grown. Maren, next morning: "What did you dream?" — Purpose: clothing; her yearning spoken; Maren watching.

### Part 3: Work and Tools (beats 18 to 27, under an hour)

Mechanics: gathering with tools, carry weight, tiredness, crafting with wood and stone, building a hut, animals and cooking, farming.

18. The first short yield. Kira: "The soil is tired. It was never tired." The Rite of First Cut: a stone blade, a basket. Weight, sweat, tiredness. — Purpose: tools; the soil tires.
19. Thera teaches at dawn. "You used to hear it without trying. Now you must sit still. You cannot hold what you have not joined." You heal a sick sapling. "There. You felt it." Quieter: "Too fast." — Purpose: resonance with a heavy body; her gift as a teacher.
20. Building. Brennan fells a tree; logs on your back; you raise a hut. Thera brings the first meal to your hearth and eats it with you. — Purpose: the hut; Brennan's hands; a quiet before the weight.
21. The Rite of First Blood. Brennan's snare at the Watering Hollow; the Tenders don't look. The beasts are thinner, showing teeth. Kira cooks it and does not eat. — Purpose: animals, cooking; the beasts coarsen faster than the people.
22. Sowing with Kira: seeds that need tending now, a season that turns. The Blight reaches the fields. Dov, heavier, eats standing. — Purpose: farming; the Blight spreads; Dov's fall deepens.
23. The Three Roots stone at the Deep threshold. First: the seed seeks soil, no shame. Second: the tree dreams of being soil again, quiet, still. Third: the roots hold the ground for others; not rest, not reaching, remaining. Thera reads the second and goes pale: "I've dreamed of it. So many times." — Purpose: the teaching in the world's words; her yearning named.
24. Seren. Brennan carries water into the caves; you and Thera follow. She is luminous and almost gone. "I was to be the Heart. At the root I saw the turning: every seeded world grows walls and burns. I will not plant one more field for the fire. I am going out like a lamp. It is not fear. It is the end of thirst." Thera: "Is that wrong?" Seren: "Ask the ones who stay." You answer her, or you don't. — Purpose: the Second Root with a living face, honoured; Thera's yearning now comes from seeing; a leaning choice.
25. Crisis day: the Blight-sick, the hungry, the cold. Behind the Healing Grove: still, staring at nothing. "I can't hear myself. Only them." You sit. Say nothing. — Purpose: the mask slips.
26. "I don't know how to need people. I only know how to be needed." Then: "All my life I listened. You're the first one who listened back." — Purpose: she lets you see her.
27. The Small Grove, never shown to anyone; the flowers lean to her; a real laugh. The pact: "Something is wrong and they know. We find out together. Not yet." — Purpose: intimacy; co-conspirators.

### Part 4: Discovery (beats 28 to 37, about 45 min)

Mechanics: the Edge, hidden passages to the hull, the membrane sky, the revelation. Resonance has thinned to a whisper.

28. The summons. Maren: "The Grove is more than it appears." Thera: "The Rootsong is afraid." "Go to the Deep. Stay away from the Edges." After: "I can't just listen. I need to SEE. Come with me." — Purpose: pressure; her resolve.
29. Past Brennan's torch into the Thinning: trees thin in a perfect gradient; the ground is not soil; under the peeled moss, a cold rib. — Purpose: the Edge.
30. The membrane: the sky curves to the ground; a seam; your face in it. A crack: cold light, stars, and a brown sphere. The sphere from your dream. — Purpose: the sky is a skin; the dream paid.
31. The Rootsong floods through Thera: the Hearth burning, the leaving, two hundred years alone. She falls; you catch her. "She's been carrying us. She's so tired. And we came all this way so it can burn again." — Purpose: Lira as a weight; the cycle seen.
32. Brennan: "You saw. I should have stopped you. I never can." The Watchers' way: a ribbed corridor, doors that sigh, a cold room of cradles. One open: yours; its plate bears the trowel's script. The rest sealed. — Purpose: origin; the hull.
33. The High Terrace. Maren: "There is no dark lord in our woods. The shadow you see is your own hardened body. You learned to hunger, so the fruit must rot. We did not fall. We came down. Every rite was a stair. Below us is a dead world, and the ones after need a grove on it." — Purpose: the twist; the Third Root as the ship's own choice.
34. Maren at the Deep threshold, next day. "The last stair is a person. Lira let go of the Hearth and has held us ever since; she cannot also land us. One goes all the way down into the stone, so the Tree can root, or the ship arrives and shatters. Seren was asked. She chose to go out. I have waited thirty years for another." — Purpose: the requirement, paced; Seren's choice placed with respect.
35. The records in Elder Hall. Your file: "Witness-class. Preserved." A note in Lira's hand: "I sent them dreams, so they would know her voice." Tomas at the door, lucid: "Lira's Promise. That's her name. Because Lira promised. And you will too." — Purpose: the witness; the ship's name; the prophecy.
36. "Thera collapsed. In the Deep. She went alone." The Heartroot Chamber: roots branching like thought, light pulsing. "Thera. Listen to MY voice." Your memories together. She returns. — Purpose: you save her.
37. The Small Grove again. "I always knew. I didn't want to know." Her dream spoken: "A morning no one needs me. A field I tend because it's a field. That's all. I never said it out loud." A pressed flower: "Remember I wanted to stay." You can no longer hear the Pulse here. "I need to understand what I'm becoming." — Purpose: what she gives up, plainly and without romance; resonance nearly gone. (owner 2026-10-30)

### Part 5: Descent (beats 38 to 48, about 45 min)

Mechanics: the vow, fear, the first danger and combat, wounds and scars, the landing as a phased world event, grief.

38. The Heartwood, everyone, the sky stuttering. Maren: "We are a ship. We are coming down on purpose. One of us must be the last stair." Thera: "I'll do it." Dov: "It started when the sleeper came." — Purpose: the choice made in public; blame.
39. "Take me instead." Maren: "It has to be a Rootspeaker. The ship chooses." Lira, through the roots, to you alone: "You were sent to remember. If you take her place, who tells the tale?" — Purpose: the witness's place.
40. The Vow of the Roots. Maren asks who will sit in the root-alcoves and spark the rooting. Tomas stands first: "I was kept for this." Then others, dozens. — Purpose: the Vowed; Tomas's purpose.
41. Fear. The Pulse goes erratic; the Blight burns through a grove in a day; the beasts come out of the Eastern Grove. A wolf-thing at the Hollow. Brennan puts a staff in your hands. The first fight, the first wound, blood that is yours. Thera binds it; the scar stays. — Purpose: violence last; the body that bleeds.
42. Dov at the Deep threshold, trying to stop the Vowed, strikes Thera once; she staggers, no more. You hold him back or strike him; either leaves weight. Brennan: "Enough. She is choosing to save us. Let her choose in peace." Kira: "There has to be another way!" Maren: "I've been saying goodbye to her for twenty years." — Purpose: craving's end; a leaning choice; Kira's love of the world as it is.
43. The last night. She comes to your hut with her herb bag and empties it onto your table: which leaf for fever, which for a wound that will not close, how to sit with the dying. "When they're sick after, do this." She asks nothing. At the door: "Some paths are walked alone." You go with her to the Deep threshold and keep vigil through the night, or you let her go alone as she asked. Nothing is said about what you are to each other. — Purpose: the bond shown only in what she hands on; a leaning choice. (owner 2026-10-30)
44. At first light the roots call you down, whether you kept vigil or not. The Heartroot with her. Lira: "I was waiting for you. When you look for yourself, what do you find?" "I can't find her." "Seren's way is a true way. The thirst ends. I will not call you back from it. Rest is not false. It is only alone." She wavers; the pull is real. Then, looking at you, at the Tenders: "The love is still here. It can't rest while they're lost." — Purpose: wisdom and compassion both land; the offer is good.
45. The ship phases in. The membrane goes clear; a yellow, poisoned sky; the Grove tilts; weight like a hand on your back. The Vowed go to their alcoves; Tomas touches the statue: "I'll ask her myself." Brennan comes up from the caves: Seren's lamp has gone out. No smoke. — Purpose: three ways in one hour: out, down, down and remaining.
46. Her hands on the root. "You were the first voice that listened back. I looked for myself and couldn't find her. Isn't that strange? If you ever look for me and can't find me... that might not be as sad as it sounds." She laughs. Light. Gone. Impact: the taproots drive down, the alcoves blaze gold, the Tree roars, the hull splits. Where the Vowed sat, white ash; beside the root, her pendant. — Purpose: the Heart and the landing.
47. Grief. The chamber is warm and she is not there. The survivors in the broken hull: Maren, Brennan, Kira, Dov silent. Kira counts the ash. At Lira's cracked statue, Maren: "Dov fell. The rest of us came down. The difference was only in the choosing." You speak. — Purpose: grief; the teaching closed without a lecture.
48. Kira steps first through the fractured hull, blade in hand, and you follow onto the alien stone: real stars, wind that smells of ash. Under your feet, through the dead stone, a warmth. The Pulse is strong today. "Listen for me." The Chronicler names the first step of the second wave. End. — Purpose: the hook; the First Root leads the second wave. (owner 2026-10-30)

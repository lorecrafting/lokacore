# Culture template

Status: stub. Area: [Peoples, species and cultures](../README.md#07-peoples). Canon tier: none yet.

**Purpose.** The fixed questions every culture answers (after Wrede's questions), so cultures are comparable and complete.

**Must answer.**
- Values, taboos, greetings, dress, food, housing, family, work, law, faith, arts, war, death?
- How do they treat strangers, magic, the order, money, the dead?
- What do they get wrong about the world, and what do they know that others do not?

**Must agree with.** [Manners, greetings and taboos](../16-daily-life/manners-greetings-and-taboos.md), [Religions roster](../09-religion/religions-roster.md), [Naming guide: people](../08-languages/naming-guide-people.md), [Crafting traditions by culture](../14-crafting/crafting-traditions-by-culture.md).

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Sutra Structure: The Opening Praises & The Great Dedication
The entire narrative arc of Loka is structured like a classical sacred sutra:

1. **The Opening Invocation (Praises of the Great Beings):**
   The story opens following the opening line (*"In the beginning of this great eon..."*) with a solemn, poetic chant singing praises of the Great Awakened Beings (the Bodhisattva equivalent):
   - Those who possess boundless compassion, who refuse the solitary peace of higher realms to descend into the burning house of the coarse worlds.
   - The silent steersmen of the void, the martyrs of the deep roots, and the guardians of the fragile seedlings.
2. **Player Agency within the Inevitable Wheel:**
   - *What Cannot Be Changed (The Cosmic Wheel):* The cosmic autumn cannot be forced back into summer; the eon will inevitably slide toward dissolution. The entire planet cannot be turned into a global utopia.
   - *What The Player Changes Permanently:* The survival of the sanctuary, the purity of the seedlings, the unknotting of karmic vendettas between warring lineages, and whether individual souls fall into darkness or lift back into the light.
3. **The Final Page: The Great Dedication of Merit (迴向文):**
   The game concludes not with triumphalism or self-congratulation, but with a **long, heartfelt Dedication**:
   - Every furrow dug, every wound borne with forbearance, every act of restraint, and every syllable of the Sacred Remembrance is dedicated outward to all beings across all spheres:
     > *"By whatever virtue has been gathered through this passing across the void,*  
     > *May every being burning in the feuds of the heavy worlds find cooling water.*  
     > *May those wandering in darkness perceive the uncreated light.*  
     > *May the hungry taste the sweet earth of peace, and the fearful lay down their iron.*  
     > *As long as space endures, and as long as living beings remain,*  
     > *So long may we abide to guard the seeds of the coming dawn."*

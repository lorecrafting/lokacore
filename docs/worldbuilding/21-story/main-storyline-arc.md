## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### Act I: The Grove of Descent (The Experiential Fall)
1. **The Luminous Awakening:** The protagonist manifests apparitionally (mind-born) in what appears to be an ancient, sacred forest habitat. There is no hunger, no cold, no weight.
2. **The Step-by-Step Coarsening:**
   - *The First Taste:* Eating the sweet sap/dew introduces physical hunger and digestion.
   - *The Cold Wind:* The need for shelter and garments arises.
   - *Work and Tools:* Clearing brush, carrying wood, and crafting introduce physical stamina, muscle fatigue, and inventory weight.
   - *Comradeship:* Training and surviving alongside your close friend/companion creates deep emotional bonds.
3. **The Seer's Revelation:**
   The Seer atop the terrace reveals that this gradual loss of luminosity is not a curse or accidental decay—it is the **deliberate navigation ritual** required to drag the ship's frequency down to match the heavy vibration of the target dead planet.
4. **The Divergence of Vows:**
   As the descent nears, the companion hears the pulse of the World Tree and accepts the *Vow of the Roots* (to be one of the 300 sacrifice-acolytes). The protagonist pleads against it, but she freely embraces her duty to ignite the root-fire.
5. **Phase-In and Crash-Landing:**
   The collective density threshold is reached. The Forest Ark suddenly **phases into view** in the toxic upper atmosphere of the dead world. Cosmic lightness collapses into violent planetary gravity. The ship crash-lands into the crust.
6. **The Rooting:**
   The 300 acolytes sit in their root-alcoves. The tectonic impact roars; their conscious Qi transmutes into the root-spark. The companion dissolves into golden light, leaving only fragrant white ash and her carved leaf token.
7. **The Reseeded Sanctuary:**
   The protagonist steps out through the fractured hull onto the alien stone. The Tree creates a living pocket-grove in the vast dead expanse. The Vanguard secures the sanctuary; the long labor of cultivating the seedlings for the next eon begins.

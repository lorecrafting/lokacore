# Metaphysics: what is real

Status: stub. Area: [Cosmology and metaphysics](../README.md#01-cosmology). Canon tier: none yet.

**Purpose.** The rules under the rules: souls, fate, free will, identity, what a person is, what a thing is.

**Must answer.**
- Do souls exist, are they one per body, and can they move, split or be made?
- Is fate real, partly real, or a belief, and does the world ever confirm it?
- What makes a person a person (speech, soul, choice) and do monsters or made things qualify?
- What is true memory and does the world keep a record of the past independent of witnesses?

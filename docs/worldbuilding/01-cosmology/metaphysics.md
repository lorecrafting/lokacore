# Metaphysics: what is real

Status: draft. Area: [Cosmology and metaphysics](../README.md#01-cosmology). Canon tier: draft (owner-directed 2026-10-16).

**Purpose.** The rules under the rules: souls, fate, free will, identity, what a person is, what a thing is.

**Must answer.**
- Do souls exist, are they one per body, and can they move, split or be made?
- Is fate real, partly real, or a belief, and does the world ever confirm it?
- What makes a person a person (speech, soul, choice) and do monsters or made things qualify?
- What is true memory and does the world keep a record of the past independent of witnesses?

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### Frequency Matching and Dimensional Phasing
1. **The Vibration of Density:** Reality is organized by vibrational resonance. Higher celestial realms are subtle, fluid, and self-radiant; terrestrial planetary bodies are coarse, slow, and heavy. A vessel existing in high resonance cannot interact with or touch a heavy-matter world—it would pass through it intangibly or shatter from harmonic dissonance.
2. **Devolution as Navigation:** 
   To cross from the upper spheres and phase into view of the target planet, the ship's inhabitants must undergo a **deliberate experiential devolution**. The crew's consciousness and physiology must step down their vibrational frequency to match the gross, dense frequency of the target planet.
3. **Ontogeny Recapitulates the Cosmic Fall:**
   Aboard the Forest Ark, the protagonist begins as an apparitional (mind-born), self-luminous presence. Through subtle, guided actions (tasting the sweet sap, feeling physical cold, gathering possessions, exerting physical labor), the protagonist's body calcifies into gross flesh and blood.
4. **The Trigger for Phase-In:**
   Only when the inhabitants' collective frequency drops to match the exact density of the dead planet does the ship achieve harmonic lock. At that precise threshold, the vessel **phases into physical view** in the planet's sky, enabling physical impact, crash-landing, and the Rooting into the planetary mantle.
5. **The Seer as Frequency Harmonizer:**
   The Seer does not merely recount ancient myths of the *Aggañña Sutta* as distant history; the Seer monitors the biological and spiritual coarsening of the novices. The sutra is the flight manual: understanding each stage of the fall is how the navigator knows the vessel is descending through the density gradients of the cosmos.

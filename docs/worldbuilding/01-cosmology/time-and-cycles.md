## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Great Eon and Cosmic Cycles
1. **The Four Phases of an Eon:** Following the ancient cosmological rhythm, each eon unspools across four great epochs:
   - *Formation (成):* The mind-born dawn, subtle light, and condensation of the sweet earth.
   - *Abiding (住):* The golden age of the Great Harmony (Da Tong) and early righteous kingdoms.
   - *Decay (壞):* The rise of private hoarding, dynastic walls, and the Warring Spheres.
   - *Emptiness / Dissolution (空):* The complete exhaustion of matter and spirit, returning to cold void before the next eon stirs.
2. **The Dharma-Ending Age (末法):**
   The world is currently in the twilight of this eon. Cosmic decay is an inevitable tide that cannot be turned back by force of arms or imperial ambition. The slide toward emptiness is structural.
3. **The Philosophy of the Grove Sanctuary:**
   Because the decay cannot be halted globally, the Central Tree does not attempt to green or heal the entire dying planet. It anchors a **Luminous Refugium / Pocket Grove**—a sanctuary of virtue and living breath holding back the ash-wastes.
4. **Seedlings for the Next Eon:**
   The mission of the Forest Ark is not to create a permanent earthly paradise, but to **preserve the seed-flame through the cosmic winter**. The crops, trees, and virtuous disciplines cultivated in the Grove are seedlings engineered to sleep through the dissolution and sprout as the first life of the *next* eon when the wheel turns again.
5. **Sacrifice as the Ignition Spark:**
   Because the eon is old and cold, the Tree cannot root through mechanics alone. The willing sacrifice (捨身) of the 300 Acolytes and the protagonist's companion provides the refined conscious Qi that bridges the gap between dead stone and living wood.

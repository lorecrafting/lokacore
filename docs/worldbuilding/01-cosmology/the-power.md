# The Power: the cosmic force behind the wardens

Status: stub. Area: [Cosmology and metaphysics](../README.md#01-cosmology). Canon tier: none yet.

**Purpose.** The category stub for the cosmic force the warden order draws on (unnamed): what it is, where it sits in the cosmos, and how it differs from ordinary magic.

**Must answer.**
- Is it a field, a will, a residue of creation, a living thing, or several of these in different beliefs?
- Is it the same source as ordinary magic, a different source, or the source of all sources?
- Does it have sides, moods or an agenda, and can it be wrong?
- What does it want, if anything, and what does it cost those who touch it (Sanderson's second law)?
- How many beings can hold it at once; is it finite (Realm scarcity)?

**Purpose.** The category stub for the cosmic force the warden order draws on (unnamed): what it is, where it sits in the cosmos, and how it differs from ordinary magic.

**Must answer.**
- Is it a field, a will, a residue of creation, a living thing, or several of these in different beliefs?
- Is it the same source as ordinary magic, a different source, or the source of all sources?

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Primordial Resonance (The Uncreated Source / The Great Luminosity)
1. **Nature of the Power:** It is not an energetic battery, an elemental fuel, or a manipulative force like ordinary magic. It is the **Primordial Resonance (The Uncreated Harmonic)**—the timeless, unconditioned ground of pure awareness and luminosity before the sweet earth was tasted and the cosmic fall began.
2. **Difference from Ordinary Magic:**
   - *Ordinary Magic:* Manipulates the coarse, separated elements of heavy matter (fire, earth, water, lightning, kinetic force). It remains trapped in causality, generating karmic backlash and physical heat.
   - *The Primordial Resonance:* Reverses causality. It dissolves karmic sediment, clears mental obscurity, and harmonizes discordant frequencies back into stillness.
3. **The Four Methodologies of Return (Ascending from the Chaotic Era):**
   In the depths of the Warring Dynasties / Dharma-Ending Age, practitioners lift themselves out of the heavy frequency through four progressive disciplines:
   - **The Vows of Restraint (Precepts / 斷緣):** Non-theft, non-slaughter, truthful speech, non-hoarding. Halts the creation of new karmic debt.
   - **Mountain Fasting & Breath Refinement (Internal Alchemy / 煉形):** Retreating to high mountain peaks, subsisting on pure celestial dew, regulating the breath to cleanse coarse planetary minerals from the blood.
   - **Mind Observation (Stillness / 止觀):** Direct observation of mental fluctuations, dissolving the illusion of "I and Mine", unknotting emotional grudges.
   - **The Sacred Remembrance (The Name of the Unoriginated / 念誦本名):** The supreme method of the darkest age. When strength fails and karma is crushing, continuously holding and repeating the resonant Name / Syllable of the Primordial Light acts as a cosmic tuning fork, instantly washing away deep karmic knots and bridging the soul back into the Uncreated Source.

# Creation and origin myth

Status: draft. Area: [Cosmology and metaphysics](../README.md#01-cosmology). Canon tier: draft (owner-directed 2026-10-16).

**Purpose.** How the world began, as the world tells it and as it was (two answers that may differ).

**Must answer.**
- What is the true account, and which stubs may know it (secrets tiers)?
- Which creation accounts do the peoples tell, and what does each get wrong?
- What was made first, what was made last, and what was never made (and so is wrong)?
- Where does the "great eon" of the opening line sit: the first eon, the current eon, one of many?

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The true account: The Descent from the Radiant Spheres
In the beginning of this great eon, the ancestors were not born of the womb, nor did they crawl from mud. They were **mind-born (apparitional / 化生)** beings descending from the upper, subtle celestial spheres:
1. **The Luminous State:** They had no gross organs, no physical gender, and no graves. They were self-luminous, gliding through space, nourished purely by meditative bliss.
2. **The Taste of the Sweet Earth:** Over eons, as subtle cosmic waters cooled, a sweet, fragrant crust formed over gross matter. Spurred by curiosity and craving, beings tasted it. Their bodies coarsened, hardened, and lost their innate inner light.
3. **The Coming of the Sky and Seasons:** When their inner radiance died, darkness terrified them. To measure time and separate day from night, the sun, moon, and stellar constellations ignited in the heavens.
4. **The Devolution of Society:**
   - Hoarding: Self-growing food was replaced by wild grains; beings hoarded days in advance, causing food to sprout husks and require brutal physical cultivation.
   - Separation into Sexes & Homes: Waste and digestion created sex organs; lust arose; shame led to the building of the first walls and dwellings.
   - Property, Crime, and Statecraft: Fields were divided ("mine and thine"); theft and lying began; the people elected **Mahāsammata** (The First King / Great Chosen One) to govern with justice in exchange for a share of grain.
5. **The Dynastic Wars & Devastation:**
   Over eras, even the covenant of the First King decayed. Civilizations descended into grasping warlords and hegemons who stripped worlds to ash, leaving planets dead, irradiated, and barren.

### The Great Eon of the opening line
"In the beginning of this great eon..." marks the current cosmic cycle. It is one of countless unspooling eons. The story takes place in the late autumn / twilight of this eon (the Dharma-Ending Age), where the Forest Ship represents a desperate, sacred effort to carry the uncorrupted seed-light to a dead world before the final cosmic night.

# Structure of the cosmos

Status: draft. Area: [Cosmology and metaphysics](../README.md#01-cosmology). Canon tier: draft (owner-directed 2026-10-16).

**Purpose.** What exists at the largest scale: one world or many, planes, the shape of the heavens, and what lies beyond.

**Must answer.**
- How many worlds or planes are there, and can anything cross between them?
- Is the world a sphere, a disc, a shell, something else, and does it matter in play?
- What is the sky made of (stars as suns, as souls, as holes), and who in-world knows?
- What is the first thing a traveller meets that proves the cosmology is real?

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### Subtle Spheres and Heavy-Matter Worlds
1. **The Gradient of Reality:** The cosmos exists along a spectrum from high, subtle, resonant spheres (where form is fluid, mind-made, and self-radiant) down to dense, heavy-matter terrestrial worlds (where gravity is crushing, form is calcified, and Yin and Yang are sharply separated).
2. **Mind-Technology and Phasing:** Crossing between the upper resonant spheres and the lower worlds does not use mechanical warp engines; it uses **mind-technology and sacred resonance rituals**. Vessels like the Forest Ark literally **phase into view** in the planet's sky, dropping from subtle vibration into physical gravity.
3. **The Crash-Landing / Violent Grounding:** Entering the dense, polarized matter of a dead world forces an uncontrollable drop in vibrational frequency, causing the ship to crash-land. This impact is the deliberate method by which the Central Tree's taproots penetrate miles into the planet's mantle.
4. **The Central Tree (Jianmu / The Axis Mundi):** The Tree mediates between Heaven Energy (*Tian Qi*, stellar winds and celestial rays caught by the high canopy) and Earth Energy (*Di Qi*, molten magma and tectonic magnetism channeled by the taproots). Where they meet, matter is alchemized into living soil and breathable air.
5. **The Two Waves of Inhabitants:**
   - **The Vanguard (The Ark Crew):** Subtle, semi-luminous beings who emerge from the ship to anchor the sanctuary and build the initial settlement. Because the planet's energy is overwhelmingly coarse, abrasive, and dense, they cannot long endure direct contact with the planet's fallen environment or degenerate native survivors. They must retreat to hidden sanctuaries (caves, deep-root vaults).
   - **The Second Wave (The Womb-Born):** Because the planet's matter is too dense for apparitional / mind-born arrival, the future inhabitants must be **womb-born (胎生)**—incarnating into physical biology to adapt to the heavy elements and steward the new civilization from within the soil.

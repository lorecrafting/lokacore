# Source and access: who can use magic and how it is learned

Status: draft. Area: [Magic and power systems](../README.md#02-magic). Canon tier: draft (owner-directed 2026-10-16).

**Purpose.** Where power comes from and who may reach it: birth, study, initiation, place, bargain.

**Must answer.**
- Is access inborn, learned, granted, or bought, and does it differ per system?
- Can anyone become a mage with enough practice (use-based) or is there a gate?
- How do origin and hometown gate access (LegendMUD gating by origin)?
- What does a first contact with power look like in play?

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### Access as State of Mind (The Gradual Unveiling)
1. **Not a Bloodline Aristocracy:** True resonance with the Power is not inherited through royal blood or purchased with gold; it is **use-based and mind-gated**. Every sentient being possesses the seed of original luminosity.
2. **Access Gated by the Gradual Path:**
   - *Coarse Elemental Arts:* Anyone in the heavy world can learn crude, combustion-based elemental manipulation through physical exertion and rote study.
   - *Subtle Resonance (Warden Arts):* Accessible only as the mind climbs the gradual stages of the path (restraint $\to$ renunciation $\to$ compassion $\to$ wisdom). A mind clouded by wrath, greed, or territorial pride literally cannot harmonize with the higher frequencies.
3. **The First Contact in Play:**
   First contact with the Power does not look like throwing a fireball. It occurs in a moment of selfless stillness—holding one's breath to spare an injured beast, sharing one's last flask of water with an enemy, or meditating at dawn on the High Terrace until the inner chatter stops and the singing pulse of the World Tree is heard in the chest.

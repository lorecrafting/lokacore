## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### One-sentence premise
A generation of pioneers aboard a living forest ark—unaware their ancestral grove is a vessel travelling across the void—phase into a ruined, heavy-matter world to crash-land and root their central World Tree, sacrificing their subtle forms so that womb-born descendants may inherit a seeded sanctuary in the twilight of an eon.

### The four pillars
1. **The Inversion of the Fall (Sacrifice over Hoarding):** 
   Civilizations fell because beings hoarded the sweet earth, built walls, and let their virtue curdle into violence. The reseeding succeeds only through the opposite: conscious, willing sacrifice (捨身) of individual form so that future life can sprout.
2. **Heavy Matter and Two Waves (Yin-Yang Polarization):**
   The cosmos moves from subtle, mind-born, self-luminous spheres into dense, heavy, coarse planetary matter where Yin and Yang are sharply divided. The subtle Vanguard crash-lands, seeds the grove, and retreats into sacred hiding spots; the second wave must be womb-born (胎生) to survive the gravity and build the new world.
3. **The Sanctuary in the Twilight (Dharma-Ending Realism):**
   This is the Dharma-Ending Age (末法). The World Tree does not magically terraform the entire dead planet; it anchors Heaven and Earth energies to sustain a localized sanctuary (a grove-refugium) holding back the toxic wastes, guarding seedlings for the *next* cosmic age.
4. **Show, Don't Tell (Environmental Truth):**
   No preachiness or moral lectures. The physical world reflects the moral state of consciousness: barren wastes, poisoned aqueducts, and automated war relics silently attest to the greed of past dynasties. The player discovers truth through tactile archaeology and lived experience.

### What this world is explicitly not (Anti-inspirations)
- **Not Western high-fantasy feudalism:** No generic European lords, elves, or taverns; aesthetic and philosophical roots draw on the Buddhist *Aggañña Sutta*, Confucian social decline (*Da Tong* vs *Xiao Kang*), and Chinese mythical resonance (*Jianmu* / Heaven-Earth mediation).
- **Not hard-tech sci-fi:** Space travel is mind technology, resonance, and sacred rituals phasing across spheres, not reaction engines or metal hyperdrives.
- **Not a global conquest power-fantasy:** The protagonist is a gardener and warden of the long twilight, not an emperor conquering the globe.

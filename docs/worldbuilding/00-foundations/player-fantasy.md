## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Three Core Player Fantasies
1. **The Pioneer of the New Soil (The Tiller / Founder):**
   Mastering essential physical survival—farming, timber, masonry, and herbalism—not as mundane chore-work, but as sacred acts of stewardship that break ground on a dead world and honor the acolytes' sacrifice.
2. **The Witness of the Great Cycle (The Cosmological Seeker):**
   Experiencing the deep time transition from ethereal luminosity down into heavy matter, uncovering through silent archaeology the ruin of past dynastic civilizations, and realizing that human consciousness directly shapes the physical world.
3. **The Guardian of the Seedlings (The Hidden Warden):**
   Resisting the cynicism and violence of the wasteland outside the grove, protecting the fragile sanctuary during the long twilight of the Dharma-Ending Age, and guarding the genetic and spiritual seeds destined to awaken in the next eon.

### What the First Hour Delivers vs. What is Withheld
- **Delivered in the First Hour:** The breathtaking, pristine beauty of the Luminous Grove; weightless movement; the first taste of sweet sap; early camaraderie with your childhood companion; learning basic gathering and crafting.
- **Deliberately Withheld:** That the grove is a ship hurtling through the void; that the darkening sky and growing decay are caused by the player's own increasing physical density; that the 300 acolytes (including your companion) will dissolve during landing; that the world outside is a scorched, ruined wasteland.

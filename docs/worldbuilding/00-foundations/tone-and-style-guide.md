## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Dominant Register: Melancholic Compassion & The Sigh of the Plight
1. **The Lament of Chaos (悲憫 / 浩嘆):**
   During the chaotic eras of the Warring Spheres, the prose register carries an aching, dignified sorrow. It observes the plight of mortal life—displaced families, broken oaths, the folly of warlords carving up dead earth—with a deep, heavy sigh. It avoids cynical nihilism, edgy splatter, or cheap shock value; it reads like the classical laments of ancient poets standing amidst imperial ruins, grieving what was lost.
2. **The Visual & Emotional Contrast (Oases of Brightness):**
   The sorrow is never unrelieved blackness. It is deliberately contrasted against **bright stars and oases of radiant goodness**:
   - A quiet mountain tea-shrine sheltering starving orphans from the rain without asking their clan loyalty.
   - An itinerant healer turning their back on a warlord's gold to hold the hand of an ordinary soldier in a ditch.
   - The quiet, unshakeable warmth of two companions sharing a bowl of hot broth beside a guttering lamp in a ruined pass.
3. **The Light That Carries Things Forward:**
   These small acts of unwavering virtue are not futile footnotes—they are the **entire point of the narrative**. The darkness exists to prove how far a single candle can shine. These oases of goodness are the living embers that keep the human spirit from turning to stone and carry history forward into the next dawn.
4. **House Rules for Prose:**
   - Sensory, grounded vocabulary: smells of damp wool, woodsmoke, cold well-water, bitter tea, singing bronze.
   - Plain rhythmic prose over purple adjectives; dignity over sentimentality.

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### Second Person Present & The Chronicler's Sigh
1. **Person & Tense:** Second person present (*"You tighten the strap of your pack; the wind off the pass carries the smell of wet soot"*). Intimate, immediate, immersive.
2. **The Chronicler's Presence:** The narrator is an unseen, ancient witness who remembers the primordial light. The voice treats the player's mortal struggles with profound tenderness, observing the folly and brutality of the chaotic era with a quiet, philosophical sigh, yet always highlighting the small, heroic sparks of virtue that keep the dark at bay.
3. **The Opening Line as the Root Key:**
   > *"In the beginning of this great eon..."*
   This line strikes the root harmonic for the entire Book. Even when the player is doing something as earthy as chopping firewood or bandaging a cut, the language carries the subtle cadence of deep cosmic memory.
4. **The Sutra Framing (Opening Praises & Closing Dedication):**
   - The Book opens as a sacred text, following the opening line with solemn praises of the Great Awakened Beings who guide, sacrifice, and guard the light.
   - The Book closes with a long, heartfelt Dedication of Merit, offering all gathered virtue to the peace and liberation of all suffering beings across the spheres.
5. **Audio Narration and the Auditory Fall from Luminosity:**
   - In prologue sequences, cutscenes, and high-tier cinematic narration, dialogue begins not as spoken grammar, but as etheric harmonic overtones in the conceptless luminosity state.
   - As cosmic waters cool and craving awakens, the sound bends into tonal, affective prosody (emotional timbre, longing, weeping, warmth) without words.
   - As density deepens, consonants and phonemic boundaries emerge, dividing the flow into recognizable human language.
   - The owner's opening line (*"In the beginning of this great eon..."*) marks the precise crystallization point where primordial overtone resonance resolves into the crisp, spoken human voice of the Chronicler.

## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Core Thematic Questions
1. **Karmic Entanglement vs. Release (How is Debt Cleansed?):**
   When harmed or betrayed by a rival or warlord, does the player strike back (deepening the karmic knot across lifetimes), or exercise forbearance, debt-repayment, and sacrificial release to wash the slate clean?
2. **Descent into Matter vs. Ascent into Light:**
   Civilization requires heavy tools, farming, walls, and iron to survive the physical world, yet every physical accumulation binds the spirit to coarse frequency. How does one live, build, and survive in the mud without forgetting the primordial luminosity?
3. **The Chaos of the Fractured Era (Power vs. Righteousness):**
   In an era of warring dynasties, shifting loyalties, and opportunistic hegemons (mirroring the Spring and Autumn / Three Kingdoms), does one conquer to impose order, or cultivate internal virtue to become an island of stillness?
4. **The Ultimate Return (The Path to the Unoriginated):**
   When the world is drowning in the Dharma-Ending Age and personal strength is insufficient, how does a being cross back into the Source—through exhausting individual effort, or through the total surrender and resonance of the Sacred Remembrance?

## Decisions (2026-10-20)

### 1. The Linguistic Fall: Three Tiers of Expression
Speech in Loka is not an arbitrary cultural invention; it is an index of cosmic density and mental division (*papañca*). The devolution of language directly mirrors the cosmological descent from the Radiant Spheres:

1. **The Primordial Resonance (The Voice of Luminosity / Conceptless Sound):**
   - **Nature:** In the subtle upper realms, mind-born beings possess no vocal cords, tongues, or lungs. Communication is pure harmonic vibration, overtone resonance, and unconditioned frequency.
   - **Absence of Concepts:** There are no discrete words, no grammatical syntax, and no dualistic separation into subject and object ("I" vs. "you"). Communication is the immediate, unmediated sharing of meditative bliss and luminosity.
   - **Auditory Texture:** Pure sustained overtones, bell-like chimes, singing bowl resonance, and ethereal glass tones.

2. **The Affective / Tonal Voice (The Arising of Feeling & Craving):**
   - **Nature:** As beings touch and taste the cooling sweet crust of matter, sensation (*vedanā*) and craving awaken. Communication slips from neutral, all-pervading harmony into directional, emotional inflection.
   - **Presence of Emotion, Absence of Syntax:** Melodic contour, pitch inflection, sighs, microtonal weeping, and humming arise. It conveys longing, awe, sorrow, or comfort, but remains pre-verbal—it has polarity, but still lacks nouns, verbs, and conceptual fences.

3. **Divided Mortal Speech (The Gross Tongues of *Papañca*):**
   - **Nature:** With the full calcification of physical bodies, gross teeth, lips, and breath emerge. Physical survival, property division ("mine and thine"), theft, statecraft, and dispute demand exact categories.
   - **Auditory Texture:** Plosives, hard stops (*p, t, k*), sibilants, and consonants chop continuous vibration into discrete symbolic tokens.
   - **The Split Mind:** Human language brings analytical precision, but at the cost of complete isolation: minds are locked in bone skulls, and words become walls, contracts, lies, and weapons.

### 2. Sacred & Secret Tongues in the World

- **The Harmonized Rites of the Warden Order:**
  The Wardens do not hoard arbitrary code-words. Their secret mantras and sacred liturgical formulas are preserved remnants of the upper tonal and resonant tiers. When chanting rites or channeling The Power, they minimize conceptual articulation, relying on sustained vowel overtones and resonant intervals to attune the mind to higher vibrational frequencies without causing mental calcification.
- **Hearing Without Understanding:**
  When a mortal hearer encounters the Primordial Resonance or high Warden overtones, the ear hears no intelligible words or grammar. Instead, the vibration acts directly upon the body and subtle channels—inducing involuntary stillness, vertigo, profound weeping, or sudden mental silence. The text log records not a transcript of dialogue, but the shift in the listener's internal state.
- **Learning and Progression:**
  The player cannot "learn" the sacred tongue by reading a dictionary or leveling an academic skill. Unlocking understanding requires spiritual and physical purification—de-calcifying the mind's grasping habit, quieting discursive concepts, and harmonizing with subtle resonance. Achieving attunement unlocks direct communion with ancient apparitional remnants, the activation of pre-fall seed-vaults, and the casting of unconditioned Warden rites.

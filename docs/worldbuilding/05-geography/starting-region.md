## Decisions (2026-10-16)

### The Forest Ark: "The Luminous Grove" (The Cradle)
The story begins aboard the **Forest Ark**, an enclosed biosphere grown around the central World Tree (*Jianmu*), travelling between celestial spheres toward a dead target planet.

### The Environmental Devolution (Pristine to Heavy Matter)
The region is dynamic. It does not stay static; it physically transforms as the protagonist levels up, learns trades, and advances the main storyline:
1. **The Pristine Beginning (The Radiant State):**
   - The grove opens as an ethereal paradise of soft, omnidirectional, pearlescent light.
   - There are no sharp shadows, no rot, no fallen deadwood, and no dirt that stains. Water is crystalline and does not chill. Footsteps leave no impression in the moss.
   - The player assumes this is an idyllic, timeless high-fantasy vale.
2. **The Subtle Coarsening (The Illusion of "Progression"):**
   - *Stage 1 (First Food):* Tasting the sap and harvesting plants introduces the first shadows behind tree trunks. Twilight appears.
   - *Stage 2 (Craft & Labor):* Cutting wood and shaping stone introduces physical friction, sweat, and stamina drain. Leaves turn yellow and fall; the scent of damp loam and decay enters the air.
   - *Stage 3 (Garments & Dwellings):* The air turns cold and damp. The player builds a wooden shelter and dons spun clothes. Night becomes pitch-black, requiring lanterns and campfires.
   - *Stage 4 (Tools, Metal & Property):* Smelting bronze/iron, trading with coins, and dividing field plots causes the mist at the grove's perimeter to thin. Beneath the peeling moss and roots, cold metallic bulkheads, structural ribs, and pressure doors of the ark's hull become visible.
3. **The Revelation of the Seer:**
   The player initially assumes a creeping corruption or dark blight is invading the grove. The Seer on the High Terrace reveals the dramatic inversion:
   > *"There is no dark lord invading our woods, traveler. The shadow you see is cast by your own newly hardened body. You learned to hunger, so the fruit must rot. You learned to possess, so the walls must rust. What you call 'advancement' is the deliberate descent of your frequency, pulling our ship down to meet the frozen earth."*

# Dong Han Medical Lecture - June 11, 2011

## Overview

This foundational lecture from the Dong Han (Eastern Scholar) medical training covers the lineage history, fundamental numeric systems, Yin-Yang theory, Five Elements, Six Energies, and introductory pulse diagnosis.

---

## I. Lineage and History

### Dong Han Pa (Dong Han Sect)
- **Dong Han** (동한) means "Eastern Scholars" - the original pure Daoists
- Claims lineage going back potentially 50,000-300,000 years through cycles of civilization
- Knowledge was "brought down" as a complete system, not developed incrementally
- The Shinra (Silla) dynasty (~7th century) is when records were reorganized
- The knowledge has been "thinning down" since its introduction

### Key Historical Figures
- **Dr. Jeon (Hong Gu Jeon)** - The instructor's teacher, described as a "living legend"
- **Dr. Cho (Cho Myung Sung)** - Founder of the first oriental medical school in Korea (Kyung Hee University)
- **Fuxi (복희)** - Ancient figure who created the original trigram chart
- **Wenwang (문왕)** - Created a practical application version of the trigrams

## IV. Yin and Yang Theory

### Core Principles
1. **Relativity** - Nothing is inherently yin or yang; always need comparison
2. **No approximation** - Be precise or stop one step before; never guess
3. **Look at manifestation** - What matters is external expression, not origin
4. **Binary calculation** - Yes/no, this/that - no in-between

### The Four Stages (四象)
| Stage | Name | Nature |
|-------|------|--------|
| Lao Yang (老陽) | Old Yang | Pure Yang (Heaven/乾) |
| Shao Yin (少陰) | Young Yin | Yang exterior, Yin interior (Fire/離) |
| Shao Yang (少陽) | Young Yang | Yin exterior, Yang interior (Water/坎) |
| Lao Yin (老陰) | Old Yin | Pure Yin (Earth/坤) |

### Critical Distinction: Shao Yang vs Shao Yin
- **Shao Yang** = Electrical/nerve heat (not measurable by thermometer)
- **Shao Yin** = Physical/metabolic heat (measurable by thermometer)
- Rule: If heat is measurable by thermometer = Shao Yin; if not = Shao Yang

## V. The Eight Trigrams (八卦)

### Fuxi Bagua (복희 팔괘도)
1. **Heaven (乾)** - Three solid lines - Pure Yang
2. **Earth (坤)** - Three broken lines - Pure Yin
3. **Fire (離)** - Yang outside, Yin core
4. **Water (坎)** - Yin outside, Yang core
5. **Wind (巽)** - Quiet surface, activity underneath
6. **Thunder (震)** - Activity on surface, quiet underneath
7. **Mountain (艮)** - Substance piling up, going upward
8. **Lake/Valley (兌)** - Deep water with activity beneath

**Key Insight**: These are VERBS, not adjectives - they represent actions/movements

## IX. Pulse Diagnosis Introduction

### Pulse Positions (Cun Kou)
- **Cun (寸)** - Distal position (beyond radius head)
- **Guan (關)** - Middle position (at styloid process)
- **Chi (尺)** - Proximal position

### Organ Positions
**Left Hand:**
- Cun: Heart
- Guan: Liver
- Chi: Kidney

**Right Hand:**
- Cun: Lung
- Guan: Spleen
- Chi: Ming Men/San Jiao

### Ming Men and San Jiao
- **Ming Men (命門)** - Life Gate - Hormonal/Endocrine system
- **San Jiao (三焦)** - Triple Burner - Central Nervous System

### Pulse Technique
1. Middle finger finds the styloid process
2. Slide just distal to it
3. Use the pad of the finger, not fingertip
4. Apply equal pressure with all three fingers
5. Press down to bone, then release to surface
6. Find the middle depth
7. Pay attention to TEXTURE, not just strength

## X. Treatment Philosophy

### Core Principle: Coexistence (共存)
- Everything in the universe is symbiotic
- Diseases are not enemies to eliminate
- Treatment seeks balance, not eradication

### The 7:3 Rule
- Tonify with force of 10
- 7 portions to vital energy
- 3 portions allowed to the "enemy" (pathogen)
- Always allow the other to coexist

### Tonification Over Sedation
- Always strengthen the body rather than fight the pathogen
- Health = tonification and letting body fight back

### Cancer as Adaptation
- Cancer cells are adaptive mutations
- Body's attempt to extend life under stress
- Not an enemy invasion but internal adaptation

## XI. Training Exercises

### Wood Sensing Exercise
- Place three fingers on wood surface for 30 minutes
- Try to sense what's underneath

### Needle Training Progression
1. Water bucket with tomato (insert without causing ripples)
2. Orange
3. Eggplant
4. Watermelon (insert until touching a seed, not poking through)

### Paper Piercing Exercise
- Use thin needle (gauge 22)
- Start with 1 page, progress to 6 pages

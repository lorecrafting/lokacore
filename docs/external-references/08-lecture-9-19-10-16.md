# Dong Han Medical Lecture - September 19 & October 15-16, 2011

## Overview

These combined lectures cover the Heart as King Organ, Double Yang/Yin conditions, hidden pulses, scattered pulses, emergency techniques, and Daoist medicine's approach to plant/herb design.

---

## I. The Heart (Imperial/King Organ)

### Key Concepts
- Heart branches everywhere through capillaries, reaching every organ
- Blood (the liquid organ) carries information throughout the system
- Heart constantly adjusts balance between organs and itself
- When heart has issues, it's often responding to problems elsewhere
- Heart is the most protected organ
- Heart issues are frequently reflective of problems elsewhere

### Tachycardia
- Fast heartbeat when it should not be elevated
- Can indicate heart problem OR be symptomatic of other conditions
- Not always the primary treatment target

## V. Zhongyang (重阳) - Double Yang Condition

### Definition
Excessive yang energy overflowing from two sources concentrating in one area

**Korean Term**: 열궐 (yeolgwol)

### Pulse Characteristics
- Slippery and long
- Higher and floating
- Large, beady, rolling texture

### Clinical Manifestations by Location
| Location | Manifestation |
|----------|---------------|
| San Jiao | In and out of consciousness |
| Heart | Very moody, sensitive, touchy |
| Liver | Constantly grouchy, anger mode |

### Critical Treatment Principle
When double yang is severe (especially in Chi position):
- DO NOT pull heat out from the top (Baihui) - DANGEROUS
- MUST pull heat downward first
- Use **Yongquan (KI-1)** to lower heat position
- Then use Zusanli to put it down
- Use Kongzui to aid
- Use Chize to cool from chest

### Baihui vs. Yongquan Function
- **Baihui (GV-20)**: Releases heat OUT of the body
- **Yongquan (KI-1)**: Retains heat but RELOCATES it from top to bottom

## VI. Zhongyin (重阴) - Double Yin Condition

### Pulse Characteristics
- Deep, short, dry, empty
- Becomes very tight from two sources

### Clinical Signs
- Blood dries up and thickens
- Blood circulation becomes difficult
- Numbness on skin
- Loss of consciousness
- False fire (caused by yin takeover/toxin)
- Skin starts rotting
- Bad rotting smell from patient
- Often caused by yeast or fungus

### Treatment Principle
- Cannot "pull cold out" - no such thing
- Must ADD HEAT IN
- Cold causes drying, freezing, crystallizing, thickening
- Must add fluidity/movement (heat)

**Important Neiching Quote:**
"In order to eliminate toxic energy, you do not pull out the toxic energy - you add in clean energy."

### Hidden Yin (隐阴)

## IX. Scattered Pulse (散脉)

### Characteristics
- No clear shape - just disconnected vibrations
- Like fog scattered on ground
- Cannot identify head, body, tail

### Significance
- Energy falling apart, disconnecting
- Organ failing
- VERY DANGEROUS - usually near death
- Patient typically dies within 12 hours
- Patient remains conscious despite failing organs

### Treatment for Scattered Pulse (All Positions)
1. Burn large moxa continuously on Guanyuan (CV-4) until pulse changes
2. Insert needles at vital points:
   - Baihui, Rentang, Renzhong
   - Shenzhong, Zhongwan, Zusanli
   - Chize, Weizhong, Hegu
   - Waiguan, Neiguan, Sanyinjiao, Shanzhong

### Critical Moxa Principle
- Air must be quiet - no wind
- Smoke must go straight up in one line
- If energy shakes, it scatters
- Cannot do moxa outside in wind

## XIX. Core Principles Summary

1. **Yin-Yang Integration**: Every condition must be understood through yin-yang dynamics
2. **Heat Cannot Be Pulled Out but Cold Must Have Heat Added In**
3. **Diagonal and Opposite Treatment**: Body's symmetry allows treatment from opposite positions
4. **Doubling Creates Critical Conditions**: Two sources of same energy = life-threatening
5. **Pulse Diagnosis Verification**: Always verify with patient questions and physical exam
6. **IV as Modern Emergency Aid**: Accomplishes what traditional medicine achieved through needle work
7. **Moxa Requires Still Air**: Energy work requires stable, unmoving environment
8. **Signature Doctrine**: Shape and form indicate therapeutic targets
9. **Multi-System Verification**: Diagnoses must make sense across binary, four-image, five-element, and six-qi systems

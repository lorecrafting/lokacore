## I. Six Qi Pulse Theory (Liu Qi Mai)

### The Six Energies

**Three Yang Stages:**
| Stage | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Shao Yang (少阳) | Young Yang | Growth stage |
| Yang Ming (阳明) | Bright Yang | Full maturity |
| Tai Yang (太阳) | Great Yang | Maximum, ready to transform |

**Three Yin Stages:**
| Stage | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Jue Yin (厥阴) | Reverting Yin | Yang shrinking, Yin growing |
| Shao Yin (少阴) | Young Yin | Yin strengthening |
| Tai Yin (太阴) | Great Yin | Maximum Yin, Yang returning |

### Important Distinction
- These terms describe QUALITY of energy, not QUANTITY
- Shao Yang is not "small amount of yang" but yang in its growth phase
- Like age stages: young, middle-aged, old

### Yang Pulse Characteristics
- Floating, Large, Fast, Long
- Indicates heat, expansion, connection with others

### Yin Pulse Characteristics
- Deep, Small, Slack, Short
- Indicates contraction, cold, loss of body fluid

## III. Fifteen Pulses of Five Seasons

### Critical Understanding
**"Normal" in these texts means "injured/affected" - not healthy.** The truly normal pulse is a perfect sine curve.

**"Seasons" Are Internal, Not External:**
- Spring, Summer, etc. refer to EMOTIONAL/MENTAL states
- Internal climate, not external weather
- This is the "secret key" to understanding classical texts

---

### Spring (Wood/Liver) Pulses

**Spring Normal (Influenced):**
- Delicate shake with tension
- Thin, tight, slow, undulating
- Like spring breeze

**Spring Diseased (Taking Over):**
- Liver attacking stomach
- Alternating between string and relaxed pulse
- Wind creates alternating/shaking quality

**Spring Death:**
- Extremely tight with strings connecting
- Feels like dry bamboo sticks
- Will contract heart muscle causing heart attack

---

### Summer (Fire/Heart) Pulses

**Summer Normal (Influenced):**
- Slippery, like round beads rolling
- "Hook" shape - round tips

**Summer Diseased (Taking Over):**
- Feels like chicken leg movement
- Like glass ball dropping down stairs

**Summer Death:**
- Feels like touching bag of beads
- Gooey at first touch, then hard and round inside

---

### Autumn (Metal/Lung) Pulses

**Autumn Normal (Influenced):**
- Soft and slender on surface
- Gets stronger as you press deeper
- Multiple layers

**Autumn Diseased (Taking Over):**
- Feels like touching chicken feathers
- Layers disconnect and separate

**Autumn Death:**
- Completely buried in water
- Cannot find pulse clearly

---

### Winter (Water/Kidney) Pulses

**Winter Normal (Influenced):**
- Soft and large with light pressure
- Becomes sharper, deeper, harder as pressed
- "Like a bird's bill"

**Winter Diseased (Taking Over):**
- Penetrates deep with cushiony sensation at end
- Creates fever response from deep cold

**Winter Death:**
- Feels like hitting small beads with fingertip
- "zzzzz" sound quality - kidney failing

---

### Mid-Summer (Earth/Spleen) Pulses

**Mid-Summer Normal (Influenced):**
- Moderate sandy pulse showing moisture
- Sticky, wet, unpleasant texture

**Mid-Summer Diseased (Taking Over):**
- Sharp pecking sensation with substance
- Internal swelling, water retention

**Mid-Summer Death:**
- Pulse extends and goes down in one line
- Penetrates to bone marrow

## V. Yin-Yang Treatment Principles

### 以热治热, 以寒治寒
**Yi Re Zhi Re** - Treat heat with heat
**Yi Han Zhi Han** - Treat cold with cold

### Correct Medical Meaning
- When heat outside and cold inside: give heat to the cold part
- When cold outside and heat inside: give cold to the cold part
- Treat the IMBALANCED area with its corresponding energy

### Practical Examples

**Flu with Fever:**
- Hot inside (fever), cold outside (chills)
- Don't heat from outside (traps cold deeper)
- Use lukewarm shower to close pores

**Frostbite:**
- Don't put frozen limb in warm water
- Put in cold water first
- Let frozen tissue become cold water temperature gradually

## I. High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)

### Body Type Classification: Taiyang
- Large body type with large shoulders, thick waist, thick neck
- Carries excessive heat in the body
- Visual signs: Red eyes, red ear lobes, flushed appearance
- Sleep issues: Difficulty due to hot feet, constantly turning pillow

### Primary Treatment Points
| Point | Location | Use |
|-------|----------|-----|
| Yangxi (LI-5) | Anatomical snuffbox | Primary for heat type |
| Kunlun (BL-60) | Foot correspondence | Balances upper/lower body |
| Yongquan (KI-1) | Sole of foot | Severe cases, 18-20 gauge needle |
| Zusanli (ST-36) | Below knee | Less severe cases |
| Sanyinjiao (SP-6) | Above ankle | Arterial hardening |

### The Pulmonary Artery Problem
- Most common blockage occurs in pulmonary artery
- Plaque builds where fire and metal engage
- When blocked, left side pressure may be lower than right
- **Key insight**: High heart pulse but low BP reading = blocked artery

### Systolic vs Diastolic Understanding
- **Systolic**: Maximum pressure when artery fully extended
- **Diastolic**: Lowest pressure where pulsation is sensible
- **Critical measure**: The GAP between systolic and diastolic
- Want gap MORE than 40
- 170/80 (gap of 90) is safer than 120/100 (gap of 20)
- Small gap = rigid arteries = stroke risk

### Categories of Hypertension

**Category 1: Taiyang/Heat-Based**
- Physical blockage from fatty deposits
- Treatment: Yangxi, Kunlun, Yongquan
- Requires dietary intervention

**Category 2: Nerve-Driven (Most Common Now)**
- Caused by stress, anxiety, depression
- Fluctuates with emotional state
- Treatment: Four Gates - Shaofu and Yanglingquan
- Can progress to heart valve problems

**Category 3: Temporary/Anxiety-Based**
- Spikes only during measurement ("white coat hypertension")
- Must measure repeatedly until patient is bored/relaxed
- DO NOT treat with pressure-lowering protocols

### Five Element Treatment for Heart Fire Excess
When fire excessive in heart (valve problems):
- **Tonify**: Shaohai (water on heart), Yingu (kidney meridian)
- **Sedate**: Rangu (kidney), Shenmen (heart)
- **Exception**: Sedate Shenmen (earth) instead of Shaofu (fire) - never directly sedate the heart

## II. Diabetes

### The Three Essential Cravings
- **Fat, Sugar, Salt** - Most delicious things to humans
- Body 100% relies on these three for survival
- Cravings are unstoppable because essential for life

### Fat-Sugar-Liver Relationship
- Fat and sugar are essentially the same - one chemistry step apart
- Liver converts between fat and sugar
- All hormones (estrogen, progesterone, testosterone) are fat-based

### Salt Importance
- Human body is salt water (saline 0.7-0.8)
- Salt enables: electrical conductivity, mineral transport, water preservation
- "We are salt water fish, not fresh water fish"
- High mineral salt essential for endocrine function

### Thirst Categories
| Type | Korean | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| Upper Thirst | 상소갈 | Craving cold water, upper body heat |
| Middle Thirst | 중소갈 | Middle burner issues |
| Lower Thirst | 하소갈 | Lower body/kidney issues |

### Diabetic Signs
- Drinking excessive water (2 gallons/day) and frequent urination
- Body trying to clean itself through kidney hyperfunction
- Losing minerals rapidly

### Diabetes as Secondary Cause
- Hypertension caused by diabetes IS DIABETES
- Cure diabetes and hypertension resolves
- Skinny diabetics: liver cannot convert sugar to fat

### Treatment Philosophy
- Fundamentally "lazy, well-eating people's disease"
- Exercise maintains body temperature and circulation
- Mild cases: 1-3 years consistent exercise and diet control
- Hot environment + activity = lower blood sugar
- Cold environment = higher blood sugar

### Diabetes Types
- **Type 1**: Genetic/juvenile - born unable to produce insulin
- **Type 2**: Acquired through lifestyle
- **DKA**: Toxicity affecting kidney function

## V. Climate, Seasons, and Pathology

### Seasonal Disease Progression
- Misbehavior in winter leads to illness in spring
- Spring: wind attacks open pores
- Summer: chance to recover
- If not recovered by summer, can fail in autumn
- Attack by one element, hurt by next element

### The Dampness-Heat Penetration Principle
- Dampness combined with heat penetrates DEEPER
- Wet towel on hot cup: initially cool, then soaks heat to hand
- 150°F water cooks deeper than 400°F oil
- Dampness INVITES heat inward

### Sweat and Yang Qi Loss
- Sweating = losing yang qi (not gaining heat)
- Red flag: stopped sweating in hot environment = kidney failure beginning
- Flu + hot shower = disaster (pores open, cold qi enters)

### Pore Management During Illness
- Cover enough to not feel cold, but not too much
- If too warm, pores stay open, keep sweating
- Peppermint soap can help close pores
- Wipe sweat starting from face (most yang energy)

### One-Sided Sweating = Paralysis Risk
- Brain must receive equal feedback from both sides
- If one side cold, brain reduces signal to other side
- Result: Paralysis
- Example: Driving with window open one side = facial palsy risk

## VI. Blood Sugar and Temperature

### Temperature-Insulin Relationship
- Skin surface warmer than inside = insulin secretion increases
- Heat inside (penetrated) = insulin NOT secreted, sugar rises
- **Minor muscles contract with hypoglycemia** (fingers, toes, face, jaw)
- **Major muscles contract with hyperglycemia**

### Practical Application
- Hypoglycemic patient cannot take hot bath (sugar drops)
- If they breathe hot steam first, THEN take warm bath = safe
- Inside/outside same temperature = no sugar fluctuation

## VIII. Headache

### Underlying Mechanism
- All headaches end as neuralgia (nerve pain)
- Tissue stressed > rigid > blocks circulation > neurotoxin accumulates

### Two Main Meridian Routes
1. **Gallbladder Channel**: Arterial circulation issues on scalp
2. **Bladder Channel**: Central nervous system/spinal issues

### Treatment Approach
1. Loosen trapezius muscle (rub the TENDON itself)
2. Loosen base of skull
3. Increase blood flow with acupuncture/moxa
4. For chronic: burn moxa directly on painful spot

### Finding Treatment Points
- Use sharp, rounded tip to locate exact painful spot
- Press in, moxa fits in depression, burn quickly
- Scalp feels "fat" and cushy in severe headache

### Occipital Neuralgia
- Caused by cervical spine damage (C1-C2)
- Pinched nerve from disc bulging
- Pain shoots from base of skull to eye
- Treatment: Find painful point on neck, burn moxa daily

### Toothache Masquerading as Headache
- Pain shoots TO temple (not from back of neck)
- Worsens with eating/biting
- Check pulse to differentiate

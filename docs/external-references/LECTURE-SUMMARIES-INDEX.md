## Core Concepts Overview

### Foundational Systems

1. **Yin-Yang Theory** - Binary calculation system for understanding all phenomena
2. **Five Elements (Wu Xing)** - Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water correspondences
3. **Six Qi (Liu Qi)** - Wind, Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Fire, Cold
4. **Eight Trigrams (Bagua)** - Directional energy expressions
5. **Twelve Meridians** - Energy channels connecting organs

### Diagnostic Methods

- **Pulse Diagnosis (Mai Jing)** - Primary diagnostic tool
  - Nine Sector System (Head-Body-Tail x Upper-Middle-Lower)
  - Twenty-Seven Sector System (Advanced)
  - Six Qi Pulse Manifestations
  - Fifteen Seasonal Pulses

- **Visual Diagnosis**
  - Aura/Energy Field Reading
  - Facial Diagnosis
  - Tongue Diagnosis
  - Skin Surface Observation

- **Other Methods**
  - Listening (voice quality)
  - Questioning
  - Palpation

### Treatment Principles

1. **Coexistence (70/30 Rule)** - Never satisfy 100%; leave room for body's own healing
2. **Tonification Over Sedation** - Strengthen the body rather than fight pathogens
3. **See-Saw Law** - Treat from the opposite side
4. **Synchronization** - Connect with target energy before manipulation

## Study Recommendations

From the lectures, the following study approach is recommended:

1. **Practice over theory** - Pulse checking and needling practice are more important than memorization
2. **Daily practice** - Check pulses on self, family, friends
3. **Required reading** - Neijing Suwen (5 pages daily)
4. **Needle training** - Tomato in water exercise, progressing to orange, eggplant, watermelon
5. **Chart everything** - Draw pulse waveforms immediately; memory fades quickly

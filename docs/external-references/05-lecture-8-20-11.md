- Must address all affected organs simultaneously

## IX. Needling Techniques

### No-Mind Needle (Basic Foundation)
**Practice Method:**
1. Fill bucket with water
2. Place tomato in water
3. Insert needle without creating any wave
4. Practice 1 hour daily as meditation

**Advanced Progression:**
1. Go in and let go (fast release)
2. Go in, hold briefly, let go
3. Go in, hold, stay
4. Manipulate water without moving needle

### Energy Manipulation Through Needle
1. Hold needle completely still in water
2. Make water spin (using mind/qi only)
3. Stop water
4. Spin in opposite direction
5. Create different wave forms

### Hair Pendulum Practice
- Hold single hair like pendulum
- Without moving hand, make it swing
- Proves mind controls qi

### Energy Disconnection
**Critical:** Must disconnect after treatment
- Mental "thunder" - sharp break in visualization
- Snap/click physically
- Wash hands in cold water
- Clear mind completely

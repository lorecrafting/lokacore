Loka Chapter One

The Fox of Ashmere, chapter one

A mock playable of the first release, set in the room view. Fifty-seven places, sixteen people, ten quests and two endings, from docs/spec/00a. It is a playtest of feel, not the engine: the rules are a small table interpreter in the page. Your place is kept in this browser; Restart begins again from the title page.

Effects

How to play

  1. Read the page

    Tap the room title to look again. Dotted words are details. Each “is here” line opens that person or thing. Italic lines are things you can do now.

  2. Walk with the map

    Hold the little map and drag toward a path, or out to the stairs for up and down. A tap opens the Map page, where you can travel to any place you know.

  3. Watch the status line

    Tap your position to sit, rest or sleep; time passes when you wait, rest or sleep. Tap the numbers for your Character and the ways to pass the time.

  4. Mind the fen

    Low water comes every six hours, for three. The fen is dark after eight, and the hounds hunt then. Keep a light, and keep your lantern filled.

  5. Or type

    Swipe up on the footer for the text drawer. Every tap shows up there as its command. Try help.

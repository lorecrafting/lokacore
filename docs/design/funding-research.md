## Vision Summary & Pitch Angles

Loka can be positioned through multiple lenses depending on the audience:

### Core Vision
An online social platform inspired by text-based RPGs that:
- Bridges digital and physical community
- Integrates real-world small business/indie maker support
- Promotes meditation, physical fitness, and slowing down
- Uses a "living book" aesthetic that encourages reading over dopamine-driven gaming
- Provides a healthy online space for younger generations
- Creates offline network effects (people meeting IRL)

### Pitch Angle Options

| Audience | Pitch Angle | Key Framing |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Social Impact Investors** | "Digital third place" for youth mental health | Counter to addictive social media |
| **Gaming Investors** | "Games for Good" / social impact gaming | Anti-grind, community-first MUD |
| **Wellness/Mental Health** | Digital wellness + mindfulness platform | Meditation integration, calm aesthetics |
| **EdTech** | Literary/reading platform disguised as game | Encourages reading, slower engagement |
| **Community/Social** | Next-gen community platform | Real-world connection, mentorship |
| **Small Business** | Indie maker marketplace with community | Support small businesses with heart |

## Pitch Positioning Strategy

### Primary Narrative

**"The Anti-Doomscrolling Platform"**

Position Loka as the antidote to addictive social media and dopamine-driven games:

> "We're building a digital third place that encourages young people to slow down, read, connect meaningfully, and build real-world relationships—all through a text-based RPG that feels like stepping into a living book."

### Supporting Narratives

1. **Youth Mental Health Crisis** (for grant applications)
   - Screen time linked to anxiety/depression
   - Your platform encourages slower, mindful engagement
   - Meditation and physical fitness integration

2. **Community Platform 2.0** (for social/community investors)
   - Real-world meetups, not just online engagement
   - Mentorship and intergenerational connection
   - Small business support creates economic value

3. **Games for Good** (for gaming investors)
   - Counter to exploitative game design
   - Social systems > grinding mechanics
   - Proven demand for "cozy" and slow games

4. **Digital Literacy + Reading** (for EdTech)
   - Text-based = reading practice
   - "Living book" aesthetic encourages literacy
   - Narrative-driven learning

### Metrics to Track

For future fundraising, emphasize:
- **Engagement quality**: Session length, return rate, depth of interaction
- **Real-world impact**: Meetups facilitated, small businesses supported
- **Mental health proxies**: Self-reported wellbeing, usage patterns
- **Community health**: Mentor relationships, retention without addiction

## Funding Landscape Reality Check

### Current Market Conditions (2024-2025)

**Challenging:**
- EdTech VC collapsed from $20.8B (2021) to ~$3B (2023-2024)
- Gaming investment recovery but "mid-range indie games" underinvested
- High interest rates reduced overall VC activity

**Favorable:**
- Mental health/wellness funding up 2,287% YoY (2024 vs 2023)
- Social impact investing growing
- Crowdfunding for games at record highs
- "Games for good" gaining recognition

### What This Means

1. **Pure gaming pitch is harder** - need social impact angle
2. **Mental health/wellness angle is hot** - emphasize this
3. **Community/social angle differentiates** - not another app
4. **Crowdfunding is viable** - 80% success rate on Kickstarter

## Recommended Approach

### Phase 1: Foundation (Now - 3 months)

1. **Build community first**
   - Discord server with early believers
   - Email list / waitlist
   - Document the journey (content marketing)

2. **Apply to accelerators**
   - DO GOOD X (Fall 2025 cohort)
   - SEED SPOT Impact Accelerator
   - Y Combinator (if ready for intensity)

3. **Pursue grants**
   - Prebys Foundation Youth Mental Health
   - Cigna Group Foundation (if nonprofit arm possible)
   - IFTF microgrants (for IF community credibility)

### Phase 2: Traction (3-6 months)

4. **Build playable demo**
   - Focus on social systems, not content
   - Show the "living book" aesthetic
   - Demonstrate meditation/wellness integration

5. **Launch Patreon**
   - Ongoing development funding
   - Build paying community before launch

6. **Consider Kickstarter**
   - When demo is compelling
   - Community is engaged (5K+ potential backers)
   - Clear milestone goals

### Phase 3: Seed Funding (6-12 months)

7. **With traction, approach VCs**
   - K50 Ventures
   - Social Impact Capital
   - Gaming + wellness intersection funds

8. **Leverage accelerator network**
   - Warm intros from mentors
   - Demo day exposure

# Monetization Ideas

> **Status:** Ideation / Brainstorming
>
> These are ideas being explored, not final decisions. To be refined with community feedback.

## Guiding Principles

### What We're Avoiding

| Pattern | Why It's Harmful |
|---------|------------------|
| Pay-to-win | Creates grind incentive, ruins contemplative feel |
| Loot boxes | Gambling mechanics, exploitative |
| Battle pass / FOMO | Daily pressure, anxiety-inducing |
| Ads | Breaks immersion, attention harvesting |
| Premium currency | Obfuscates real cost, encourages overspending |
| Energy systems | Time-gates create anxiety, not patience |
| Artificial scarcity | Manufactured urgency, speculation |
| Leaderboards | Competition where none is needed |

### What We're Embracing

| Pattern | Why It Fits |
|---------|-------------|
| Patronage | "Support what you value" |
| Creator economy | Builders rewarded for beauty, not exploitation |
| Pay for expression | Tools for creation, not power |
| Transparency | Clear pricing, honest value exchange |
| No pressure | Free tier is complete, paid is optional |

## Core Model: Tiered Subscriptions

### The Philosophy

```
Free → Full experience as visitor
Paid → Tools for creation and contribution
Higher tiers → More creative power, potential to earn back
```

**Key insight:** Each tier should potentially pay for itself at the next level.

---

### Free — Wanderer

*"Loka is free to explore"*

**Gets:**
- Full exploration of all zones
- All social features (chat, emote, sit, pose)
- Can tip builders
- Attend events
- Experience all content

**Doesn't get:**
- Building tools
- Personal space
- Ability to receive tips

**Role:** The audience. Without them, builders have no one to build for.

---

### $5/month — Resident

*"Make a home"*

**Gets everything in Free, plus:**
- Personal room (1 room)
- Template-based decoration (~50 items)
- Custom room name
- Journal (private or public)
- Small garden plot (integrates with living systems)

**Role:** Personal investment. They *live* here now.

**Why they pay:** "I have a place that's mine."

---

### $10/month — Builder

*"Create for others"*

**Gets everything in Resident, plus:**
- Multi-room space (up to 5 connected rooms)
- Expanded object library (~200 items)
- Custom ambient messages (from curated list)
- **Can receive tips**
- Listed in "Places to Visit" discovery
- Visitor insights ("12 visitors lingered this week")

**Role:** Content creators. Their spaces give free players reasons to stay.

**Why they pay:** "If 2-3 people tip me $3/month, this pays for itself."

**The math:**
- $10/mo cost
- 3 people tip $3 = $9
- 5 people tip $2 = $10 ✓
- Good builders break even or profit

---

### $15/month — Architect

*"Shape Loka"*

**Gets everything in Builder, plus:**
- Zone creation (up to 20 connected rooms)
- Full object library
- Simple NPC creation (templated, with dialogue)
- Interactive objects (doors, books, containers)
- Story fragments (discoverable lore)
- **Revenue share eligible**
- Priority in curation/discovery
- Architect recognition

**Role:** World expanders. They build what Loka becomes.

**Why they pay:** "My zone is popular. Revenue share + tips > $15."

## Template-Based Building

### The Problem

Freeform building creates:
- Moderation burden (inappropriate content)
- Inconsistent tone (breaks immersion)
- Barrier to entry (blank canvas intimidating)

### The Solution

**Not:** "Write whatever you want"

**Instead:** "Choose, arrange, personalize"

| Element | Player Choice |
|---------|---------------|
| Room type | Meditation cell, tea room, reading nook, garden alcove, hermit cave, etc. |
| Layout | 3-4 arrangements per room type |
| Objects | Pick from curated list (cushion, scroll, plant, lantern...) |
| Colors/materials | Wood tone, fabric color, stone type |
| Ambient messages | Choose from presets ("Incense drifts...", "Sunlight filters...") |
| Name | Within character limit, filtered |

### Benefits

- **No moderation burden** — all content pre-approved
- **Consistent tone** — everything fits Loka's vibe
- **Lower barrier** — anyone can make something nice
- **Still expressive** — thousands of combinations
- **Safe** — no way to write inappropriate content

### Inspiration

- Animal Crossing (arrange furniture, not write descriptions)
- Cozy Grove (aesthetic curation)
- The Sims build mode (premade assets, endless combination)

## Trusted Builder Program

For creators who want to make *custom* content beyond templates.

### The Path

```
Regular player
     ↓ (applies, shows portfolio/intent)
Apprentice Builder
     ↓ (creates with review)
Trusted Builder
     ↓ (proven track record)
Zone Architect
     ↓ (shapes major areas)
Founding Builder (rare)
```

### Capabilities by Level

| Level | Can Create | Review Process |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Apprentice** | Custom room descriptions | Every piece reviewed |
| **Trusted** | Multi-room spaces, custom ambients | Spot-checked |
| **Architect** | Full zones, NPC dialogue, story fragments | Pre-launch review |
| **Founding** | Core world areas, systems input | Collaborative |

### Benefits

- Recognition (badge, credits)
- Revenue share eligibility
- Input on world direction
- Potential paid contracts for major work

## Living World Systems

**Not just static rooms. Ecosystems that breathe.**

### Town Systems

Villages as living simulations:

| Element | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| Population | NPCs come/go based on town health |
| Economy | Shops stock based on local production |
| Buildings | Flourish or fall into disrepair |
| Events | Festivals emerge when conditions align |
| Mood | Collective atmosphere (thriving, peaceful, struggling) |

### Ecology Systems

Forests, deserts, coasts as living biomes:

| Element | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| Growth | Trees/plants mature over real weeks |
| Wildlife | Appears when habitat is healthy |
| Seasons | Different resources, different visitors |
| Balance | Over-gathering depletes, patience restores |
| Secrets | Hidden areas unlock when ecosystem thrives |

### Player Influence

**Not:** "Exploit resources for gain"

**Instead:** "Tend the world together"

- Collective action affects ecosystem state
- No individual grinding — community stewardship
- No fail state — just different states
- Changes happen slowly (days, weeks)

### Example: Village Garden

```
Week 1:  Garden overgrown → Players clear weeds
Week 2:  Soil ready → Players plant seeds
Week 4:  Sprouts appear → Villagers comment
Week 8:  Garden flourishes → New NPC arrives (herbalist)
         Bees appear, ambient messages change
Ongoing: Tended = beautiful, Neglected = overgrown
         Never "lost" — just changes state
```

### Integration with Tiers

| Tier | Interaction |
|------|-------------|
| Free | Experience, observe, small contributions (water a plant) |
| $5 | Personal plot within the system |
| $10 | Homestead that affects local ecology |
| $15 | Can design new ecosystem zones (with approval) |
| Trusted | Can create simulation rules themselves |

## Creator Revenue

### Tip Jar

- Visitors can tip creators ($1-5)
- Creator keeps 80-90%
- Optional, never prompted aggressively
- **Vibe:** Busker in a park

### Patron Subscriptions

- Subscribe to a creator ($2-5/month)
- Get notified of their new creations
- Your name on "supported by" plaque
- **Vibe:** Patreon, but in-world

### Revenue Share Pool

How it works:
1. X% of Loka subscription revenue → "Creator Pool" (maybe 20-30%)
2. Distributed monthly to eligible Architects
3. Based on **time visitors spend** in their spaces (not click-count)

**Why time-spent:** Rewards spaces people *linger* in. A peaceful garden beats a clickbait room.

**Example math:**
- 1000 subscribers × $10 avg = $10,000/mo
- 25% creator pool = $2,500/mo
- 50 active architects = ~$50/mo average
- Top architects = $200+/mo

### Community Grants

- Pool funded by Loka or patrons
- Monthly/seasonal grants to notable builders
- Chosen by community nomination or curation council
- Recognition, not competition

### Commission Board

- Players/community post requests
- "We want a tea house on the eastern ridge"
- Builders claim and create
- Requester pays (escrow)

## Anti-Patterns to Watch

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Template fatigue | Regular new templates, seasonal items |
| Builder burnout | No metrics pressure, gentle analytics |
| Wealth inequality | No "richest builder" displays |
| Content stagnation | Living systems ensure world always changes |
| Free player resentment | Free experience is genuinely complete |
| Pay-to-win creep | Regular audits, community feedback |

The map as a joystick

The minimap grows and becomes the way you walk. Hold it and drag toward a path, then let go to go that way. Drag back to the middle to cancel. A plain tap still opens the Map page. With this, the “You can go” line disappears, because the map shows where the paths lead. Try it with a mouse or finger. The Old Mill has all four cases: two open paths, up, and a closed trapdoor down.

Five answers to up and down

  • The ornament that growsVariants 1–3. At rest it’s the tiny map between long rules. Press it and it zooms up, and stair nodes appear beside it for up and down.
  • Stair nodes beside the mapYour first version, at a fixed large size. Press and the map grows. Up and down are two extra nodes to the right (or left) of the map, and you drag all the way out to one. The drag lights your target the whole way. Variants 1–3.
  • Diagonal stairsLoka is six-way, so the diagonals are free. Drag up-left or up-right to go up, and down-left or down-right to go down. Small chevrons on the diagonals show when a stair exists.
  • Hold to climbHold the map still for half a second and it lifts into a “layer” view. Then drag up to go up or down to go down. It is slower on purpose, because stairs are rarer than paths.
  • Stair keysUp and down stay as two small keys beside the map. It isn’t clever, but it can’t be misread.
  • Spiral stairCircle your thumb around the map: clockwise climbs and counter-clockwise descends. It’s the most delightful and the least discoverable.

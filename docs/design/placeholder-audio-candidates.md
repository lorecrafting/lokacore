# Placeholder audio candidates (2026-10-31)

Shortlist for the owner to pick by ear; nothing here is downloaded or in the app. Licences were read on each page on
2026-10-31; recheck when a file is bundled. Rule: [human-made content](../decisions/owner-decision-human-made-content-2026-10-31.md).
Tracking: [plan, section 14](effects-and-sound-plan.md#14-music-battle-music-and-cutscenes-proposed-2026-10-31-details-open).
Superseded in part by the [audio listening list](audio-listening-list.md), which holds the whole Scott Buckley
catalogue mapped to moments, the stinger, fanfare and log-line sound decisions, and the paper, mechanism and
combat sound candidates. What stays here: the non-Buckley music fallbacks and the sound-pack rows.

**Provenance strength.** No source states "no AI" (only a composer's claim, at most). The upload date is the best evidence
available: anything before 2022 predates usable text-to-music tools. Moods below are the authors' words or tags; none has been
listened to.

## How to pick

Listen on a phone with earbuds. For each role note: keep, maybe or no. Winners get a `manifest.json` row when
`loka-p2s8` lands. CC-BY tracks need a credits line.

A real death sets that slot's replacement due at fatal clock + **172800** logical seconds; an actual replacement allocates a new pair. Use an authored one-hour wander interval **3600**, ordinary reciprocal legal edges and C3's one control job per plan. No deer teleports across disconnected rooms. The former nine-room corridor and multi-home plan are withdrawn; the archived three-room area is initial placement, with Smithy the Orchard deer's adjacent escape refuge. No hound-area expansion or route restriction is needed.

Player entry creates or replaces at most one generation-bound, one-shot sight job for each co-present living deer, due **300** logical seconds after that entry. When a population due job makes a checked `entity.transfer` into the player's current room, that same writer group creates or resets the sight job with the exact transfer and population-job occurrence as cause; no generic arrival event is required. Re-entry resets the deadline and cancels the former occurrence. At dispatch, recheck original generation, HP, co-location, exact current job and a legal adjacent refuge within that plan's area. If still seen, transfer the original deer and its held hide stays with it. Sight first closes an open encounter and cancels its pending current round within the sight group. If a current ordinary round at the same due clock runs first, survives and advances, a later current sight job may close that exact encounter and cancel only its scheduled successor through the checked round-to-sight handoff. Keep only that matching closure/cancellation in the round group; sight completion, transfer and slot write stay in the sight group. A fatal round cancels sight; stale or blocked sight completes without movement, loot or credit and leaves a live encounter's round pending. Same-time jobs keep `(due_time, job_id)` order and other cross-group conflicts fault. Extend C4's same-clock `last_flight_at` guard to the non-pack deer slot so a population job cannot transfer it twice. The sight binding is saved and validated; there is no render-triggered scheduling, pursuit, random ecology or per-hound timer.

A hit can kill; controlled misses through the equal-due round still permit +300 live flight. Do not assert a particular RNG hit without independently fixed seed/draw evidence. Death owns one public corpse and transfers its exact held **100g** hide once. Fleeing yields no corpse, hide transfer, S1 rat credit or quest credit.

## Goal, dependencies and governing clauses

Crows visibly acquire a dropped shiny item, carry it and deliver it to a real nest in Oak Branches, from which the player can recover it. Source depends on C3 provenance/animal jobs, D5 canopy and D6's actual old coin; re-pin all three and exact custody/container eligibility before assignment. No quest/room additions. Governors: archived00a §§4/5/10/11, active containment/protected Give/death/schedule/reaction, protocol events/composition/budgets, save changed-row/reopen and C1 job-emitted acquisition carry at this actual consumer.

## PM policy and composition

Use **4 live crows maximum** across four cap-one C3-style two-room plans (two Green, one Drowned Oak, one Oak Branches), respawn **86400 logical seconds**, with a bounded legal travel corridor `village_green→well_lane→ferry_landing→reed_path→reed_bank→willow_shade→drowned_oak→oak_branches`; nest is an actual item container in Oak Branches with capacity **8 item roots** (all masses authored, not a fake detail holder). Initial nest is empty. An explicit authored allowlist initially contains **old_coin only** as the real shiny consumer. No quest/message/key/unique protected item, corpse, corpse descendant, worn/nested item or container is eligible. Source references/allowlist compatibility are validated; keywords or arbitrary “shiny” UI labels are not authority.

A committed player Drop directly in Green/Oak can bind one lowest-ID living, present, idle crow and enqueue acquisition of that **same** eligible room-root item after **150 logical seconds**. Before that job, ordinary player Take wins and the job is harmless. The acquisition is a normal exact custody transfer with crow holder and job cause; it then schedules the next transport leg after **150 logical seconds**. Each leg uses a declared ordinary adjacent exit along that at-most-eight-room corridor and re-arms once until Oak Branches; no Green→canopy teleport or general pathfinder. Acquisition from a job must not be credited as player Take or another quest's acquisition. At each leg and deposit, re-read item custody, crow life/location, nest exact identity/location/open state/capacity and job generation before delivery. Multiple crows compete through current custody preconditions; no duplicate reservation framework.

If nest fills/closes/moves before delivery, deposit the carried item into the crow's current **reachable dry room**, rather than delete, duplicate or hold it forever. After delivery or fallback, return the same crow to its authored home through at most seven adjacent corridor legs at the declared interval; do not strand a living Green crow at the canopy. A bounded **Shoo** action on a crow carrying an eligible item immediately drops the same item into that present room and starts its checked return. Accepted Attack on a carrying or returning crow makes the release if needed, cancels the due job and pauses return until the fight closes; a surviving crow resumes return, death clears it, and refused Attack leaves custody/job untouched. If the player Takes the portable nest, custody composes normally; crows stop delivering until the original nest is back/open in Oak Branches. Actual crow death drops held property through C3's corpse contract; removal/suppression must preserve it. No teleport to an unavailable holder.

The installed C3 plan admits exactly two rooms and its bundle always mints a pelt. Source must keep ordinary two-room wander, add only a proven crow corridor excursion to location/save admission, and allow a crow bundle with no companion item; do not create a fake feather or extra coin. An active transport/return excursion can occupy the corridor outside its ordinary pair, with plan wander suppressed; successful return reaches the authored home and restores ordinary two-room wander. Equal-time plan work skips a member with a due crow job in either job order.

Owners: reaction observes Drop; behavior/job owns intention/generation; containment owns all item and nest moves/capacity; population owns crow provenance; narration observes committed evidence. This is one narrow item transport invariant, not generic NPC procurement/escrow.

## Acceptance, mutants and UI/save proof

Drop the actual old coin at Green with eligible crow: one same-ID coin moves room→crow→nest on due jobs, then ordinary Take retrieves it. Taking before acquisition prevents job theft. After return, carry that same coin to Green and Drop again: the same living crow can acquire it; repeat after both Green crows have delivered to rule out permanent stranding. Populate nest with **8** literal roots: the ninth acquisition/delivery never exceeds8 and safe fallback leaves that coin accessible once. Two crows cannot both own one coin. Full-load player recovery uses existing admission; Take nest/return it preserves descendants and causes truthful source admission.

Cold-open at Drop/intention, crow-held, crow moved, nest full/closed/moved, Shoo, each return leg, combat-paused/resumed return, crow death and delivered/recovered states. At every intermediate, one container per exact item and legal job state survive, including actual COMMIT faults. A foreign quest item, protected descendant or corpse-bound root stays put. Retain30-day cap4/nest≤8/conservation output.

Plant missing source-custody precondition, capacity9, wrong holder/player acquisition credit and stale delivery after Shoo/death; each must fail controlled behavior. Browser: Drop coin→observe original crow carrying→follow to reachable nest→Take; demonstrate full nest and Shoo refusal/success without unavailable promised loot. Refresh while item is crow-held. Stop if job acquisition lacks participant/generation evidence, nest eligibility requires unbounded subtree work, or property becomes stranded on a removed actor.

## Proposed player outcome and policy

Sedge teaches herbalism in Isle Hut; Peg teaches haggle at the Chandler. Each all-hours lesson costs **2 pennies**, once, through C1's bound `skill.acquire` dialogue and exact actor-to-teacher transfer. Learning is independent of current qualification, as C1 requires. An already acquired lesson cannot charge or grant again; exact receipt replay returns its original result. These optional lessons never gate ferry, recovery, S9 or chapter completion.

Herbalism qualifies at **INT≥10 and current MV≥5**; haggle at **DEX≥10 and current MV≥5**. D12 adds the chapter's missing INT declaration/start (**proposed 10**) before using it, with thresholds in cartridge policy. C1's `stat_compare` and `resource_compare` already express this conjunction. Current MV changes qualification without erasing learning; no attribute writer, practice, XP or proficiency percentage is proposed. D11 later supplies inherited haggle and proves the same qualified purchase without paying for a lesson. Record PM selection in `docs/system` and a PM decision before source; this draft records neither adoption nor an active owner-rule change.

The herbalism consumer is the **existing Willow Shade fenwort patch**. Ordinary Harvest still transfers the lowest eligible room-held item. A learned, currently qualified actor also sees `Gather carefully`: the offered `ActionInvocation` has `action_key: "gather_carefully"`, `target_ids: [patch_detail_id]` and `input: {method: "careful"}`; it resolves to `harvest {actor_id, target_id: patch_detail_id, method: "careful"}`. Ordinary Harvest retains `input: {}` and a command with omitted `method`. Careful gathering selects the two lowest eligible IDs from B5's same twelve-item set and transfers both in one accepted proposal, with two acquisition events. Both items must remain room-held and both must fit the carrying limit at admission; stock one or insufficient capacity refuses careful gathering without transferring either item. Ordinary one-item Harvest remains available when one item fits. Drop/Take, S9 exchange, death and storage keep the same identities. No Herb Garden node, stock counter, mint, regrowth or second herb catalog is needed; D1's garden is descriptive.

The haggle consumer is **Peg's existing Buy**. For all seven authored Peg Buy offers, acquired and currently qualified haggle quotes `max(1, floor(base_buy × 9 / 10))` pennies; other buyers see the authored base. Sell remains at its authored price. The ratio and floor are chapter policy, not engine literals. Existing `quoted_price` binds the displayed Buy row to execution: the shared shelf/admission query recomputes the effective price after elapsed settlement and refuses a stale quote before transfer. A changed skill/MV state, item custody, provider presence or balance cannot silently charge a different amount. No quote token, daily counter, random roll or broad pricing framework is proposed. At the current shelf bases, qualified Buy prices are torch2, lamp_oil1, waterskin3, satchel4, iron_sword7, wooden_shield3 and spare_waterskin3 pennies; Sell stays 1/1/2/2/4/2/2. Controlled base10 quotes9, base3 quotes2, base1 quotes1. The lesson does not reserve, replenish or create any shop item.

## New Rooms — Zone by Zone

---

### HEARTWOOD EXPANSION (+6, total 18)

The heartwood is the grove's center — where the oldest trees are, where the community
gathers, where Lira's memorial stands. The expansion adds paths through the wider
heartwood, a spring that feeds the stream network, and a gathering space for community
events (the grove equivalent of a town square).

| Room Key | Name | Description Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `heartwood_east` | The Eastern Heartwood | East side of the main heartwood, where the trees are slightly younger — the grove expanded east over generations. |
| `heartwood_grove_path` | The Grove Path | A main connecting path through the heartwood, well-worn, used daily by everyone going between zones. |
| `heartwood_spring` | The Heartwood Spring | A freshwater spring emerging from between two massive roots. Feeds the stream network. Always clear. The sound of the water is distinctly different from the Pulse. |
| `memorial_approach` | The Memorial Approach | The formal path to Lira's memorial — deliberately maintained, wider than other paths, kept clear. Walking it feels like a threshold. |
| `gathering_circle` | The Gathering Circle | A cleared circular space surrounded by old trees. Not formal infrastructure — just where people have gathered for generations, and the trees have accommodated. Seating worn into exposed roots. |
| `elder_tree_hollow` | The Elder Hollow | The oldest tree in the grove, partially hollow at its base. Large enough for several people to stand inside. Used as an informal meeting place and shelter for generations. |

### THE CANOPY (NEW ZONE, +8, total 8)

*The grove has a top. No one goes there. The canopy is accessible via climbing
routes from the heartwood — the grove's tallest trees have hand-holds carved
into the bark, maintained over generations by people who needed to check on things.
The view from above is disorienting: the grove looks smaller than it feels from
inside. And the membrane — from below it is a wall; from above it is a ceiling.*

Access: up from `elder_tree_hollow` and `heartwood_spring` area.

| Room Key | Name | Description Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `canopy_access` | The Climb | The bark hand-holds. The first branches. The grove receding below. The canopy beginning above. A transition that takes effort. |
| `canopy_west` | The Western Canopy | The upper grove, western side. Looking west, the healing and orchard zones are green patches below and around. |
| `canopy_central` | The Canopy Center | The highest accessible crown of the grove's central trees. The grove visible in all directions below. The membrane visible above — closer than expected. |
| `canopy_east` | The Eastern Canopy | View east from the canopy. The Blight is visible from here as a pattern — its spread mapped in the color difference between healthy and stressed canopy. More visible from above than from inside. |
| `canopy_bridge` | The Rope Bridge | A rope-and-board bridge between two of the tallest trees, installed by someone who needed to cross without descending. Old, maintained, reliable. Swings in a way that is alarming and then fine. |
| `canopy_platform` | The Platform | A built platform between three converging crowns. Large enough for six people. Used historically for observation, for discussions that needed to happen away from below, for being alone. |
| `canopy_high` | The High Canopy | The absolute highest accessible point in the grove — the topmost branches that still hold weight. The membrane is close enough to almost touch. |
| `canopy_edge_view` | The Edge View | Where the canopy meets the membrane at the grove's boundary. Looking out through/at the membrane from height. Post-landing: this becomes a view of the open sky and the planet beyond. The most dramatic transformation of a single room in the entire landing event. |

### STREAM NETWORK (NEW ZONE, +4, total 4)

*The grove has a water cycle. The stream that runs through the understory (visible
in `stream_bed` already) has a source and a full course that isn't currently
accessible. The Stream Network adds the source and the main course as walkable rooms.*

Access: north from `heartwood_spring`, threading through `grove_pond` and down.

| Room Key | Name | Description Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `stream_source` | The Source | Where the underground water first surfaces — a crack in a root, a small upwelling, the beginning. Everything downstream starts here. The water is cold and clear. |
| `stream_lower` | The Lower Stream | The stream running through its middle section. Wider than it sounds, audible before visible. Frogs. The sound the grove makes when it's most alive. |
| `stream_pool` | The Stream Pool | A natural widening where the stream collects before narrowing again. Still water. The clearest view of the stream's health. Rootspeakers monitor this — the water carries Pulse information. |
| `stream_confluence` | The Confluence | Where the main stream meets a smaller tributary. The sound doubles. The point where both water systems become one before heading toward the understory. |

## 108 is Intentional

The grove has 108 rooms. The planet's first zone has 108 rooms. The story has 108 beats.
The number doesn't need to be explained anywhere in the game. It is simply the case.
Players who count will find it. Most won't. Both experiences are correct.

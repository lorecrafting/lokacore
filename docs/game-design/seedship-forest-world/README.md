## Overview

**Surface Level** (What players first experience):
- A lush, ancient forest
- A community of people who tend and protect it
- Something is wrong - a blight, an imbalance, a crisis

**Deeper Truth** (Revealed over time):
- The "forest" is inside a colossal seedship
- The ship has been traveling for generations
- Their mission: carry life to a dying world
- The ship is approaching its destination - or something has gone wrong

---

## Design Pillars

### 2.1 The Gradual Reveal

> "The world is not what it first appears."

Players start believing they're in a simple forest. Clues accumulate:
- Strange materials that aren't quite wood
- "Sky" that flickers at the edges
- Elders who speak of "the mission" in hushed tones
- Areas that are "forbidden" for unclear reasons

The truth: They're inside a massive seedship, a living vessel designed to restore a dying world.

### 2.2 Direction-Based Navigation

> "Learn the world by living in it."

NPCs give directions using landmarks and sensory cues:
- "Follow the stream until you hear the singing stones"
- "The healer lives where the canopy thins and the light changes"

### 2.3 Meaningful Choices (Without Lockout)

> "Your choices shape your story and reputation, but don't gate major content."

In an always-on MMO:
- Choices affect **how** you experience content, not **if**
- Reputation and relationships change based on decisions
- Some side content may vary based on past choices
- Major storylines remain accessible to all

### 2.4 Thematic Depth

> "What is worth saving? What are we willing to sacrifice to save it?"

Central questions for this world:
- What does it mean to be a "keeper" of something dying?
- How do we honor the past while building the future?
- What do we owe to generations we'll never meet?

### 2.5 Environmental Storytelling

> "The forest remembers. The walls have secrets."

- Room descriptions hint at deeper history
- Items carry fragments of the past
- The environment itself is a character

---

## Key Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **The Reveal** | Players discover the truth gradually, not through exposition dump |
| **Always-On MMO** | No "replay" - choices matter but don't lock major content |
| **Draw Them In** | Tone should hook players emotionally from the start |
| **Focus** | Design ONE world deeply before thinking about others |

## What We're NOT Doing

- Not using the monastery (that was placeholder)
- Not locking major content behind choices (MMO reality)
- Not front-loading weirdness - let it emerge naturally
- Not calling them druids

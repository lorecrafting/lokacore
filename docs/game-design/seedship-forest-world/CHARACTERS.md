## The Companion: Thera

**The Warm Flame** - the healer everyone goes to, beloved heart of the community.

| Aspect | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| **Name** | Thera |
| **Age** | Early 30s - fully formed, knows who she is |
| **Role** | Rootspeaker, the healer everyone trusts |
| **Position** | Central to the Heartwood - everyone knows her |

### Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Energy** | Warm, present, makes you feel seen immediately |
| **Voice** | Direct, honest, occasional fierce protectiveness |
| **Presence** | The person who remembers your name, asks how you're doing, means it |
| **Hidden side** | Quietly fierce - protects what she loves without hesitation |

**How she makes you feel**: Safe. Seen. Like you matter. She has a gift for full attention - when she's with you, she's WITH you.

### The Flaw

**She gives too much.**

- She can't say no
- She can't rest
- She can't put herself first
- She's slowly burning out but won't admit it
- Others take and take; Thera keeps giving
- The player sees this before Thera does

**How this shows up**:
- Interrupted meals (someone always needs her)
- Exhaustion she hides with practiced ease
- A moment where she snaps, then immediately apologizes
- The player catches her alone, finally still, looking lost

### The Dream

**A quieter life.**

- A small grove of her own
- Maybe someone to share it with
- Time to just... be
- Mornings that aren't interrupted
- Space to breathe

*She's never said this out loud. She might not even admit it to herself.*

**Why this dream is devastating**: She'll never get it. The sacrifice takes it all away. And she knows it when she chooses.

### Visual/Sensory Notes

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Appearance** | Practical clothes, worn soft. Always has herb-stained fingers. |
| **Sound** | Voice that calms. Hums while working. Laugh that surprises people. |
| **Habits** | Touches people gently when speaking. Remembers small details. |
| **Tell** | When truly exhausted, goes very still and quiet. |

## The Bonding Arc (12 Phases)

### Phase 1: First Meeting - "The Healer Finds You"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Player wakes/arrives in the Grove, disoriented, possibly injured |
| **Scene** | Thera is there. Calm, competent, asking gentle questions while checking vitals. |
| **Key moment** | "You're safe now. I'm Thera. Can you tell me your name?" |
| **Tone** | Warmth, safety, being cared for |

**What player feels**: Relief. This person is kind. I can trust them.

### Phase 2: Initial Friction - "Too Much Help"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Player is recovering, wants to contribute, explore |
| **Scene** | Thera insists player rest more. Player pushes back. Small clash. |
| **Key moment** | Thera: "I've seen people push too hard. They break." Player: "I'm not broken." |
| **Resolution** | Thera relents, but with conditions. Mutual respect established. |

### Phase 3: Thera Saves Player - "The Thinning Incident"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Player explores too far, gets into danger |
| **Scene** | Thera arrives, handles crisis with startling competence. Gets player out. |
| **Key moment** | Thera is breathing hard, spent real energy. "That was foolish. Don't do it again." But she stayed. |
| **Cost** | Thera is visibly drained afterward. She minimizes it. |

### Phase 4: Teaching - "The Rootsong Lesson"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Thera offers to teach player about the Grove |
| **Scene** | Training montage moments. Thera is patient, skilled, occasionally amused. |
| **Key moment** | Player succeeds at something small. Thera's genuine smile: "There. You felt it." |

### Phase 5: Player Helps Thera - "The Breaking Point"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Player finds Thera alone, overwhelmed |
| **Scene** | Thera is sitting, still, staring at nothing. The mask slipped. |
| **Key moment** | Player can: listen, fetch them food, handle a small task, or just sit with them. |
| **Thera's response** | Surprise. "You didn't have to..." Then, quieter: "Thank you." |

**Shift**: Thera realizes the player SEES her. Not just the healer. Her.

### Phase 6: Shared Discovery - "The Edge Together"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Something is wrong with the Grove. Thera's visions are getting worse. |
| **Scene** | Thera asks the player to come with her. To the Thinning. To look at what the Watchers guard. |
| **Key moment** | They see something they can't explain. The sky... flickers. The trees end. There's... metal? |
| **Pact** | "We don't tell anyone. Not yet. Not until we understand." |

**Bond**: Shared secret. Co-conspirators now.

### Phase 7: Player Saves Thera - "The Collapse"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Thera has been pushing too hard |
| **Scene** | Thera collapses. Actually falls. The healer who never stops, stopped. |
| **Key moment** | Player must care for Thera. Get her somewhere safe. |
| **Reversal** | Thera wakes, disoriented. "I never... this doesn't happen to me." |

### Phase 8: Quiet Moment - "The Small Grove"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Thera is recovering. She takes the player to a hidden spot - a tiny clearing she's never shown anyone. |
| **Scene** | Just... being. No crisis. No demands. Sunlight through leaves. |
| **Key moment** | Thera laughs at something small. A real laugh, surprised out of her. |

**What this is**: Intimacy. Not romantic necessarily - but real connection. Two people, present.

### Phase 9: Thera's Dream - "What I Would Want"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | Night. The Grove is quiet. They're talking, maybe couldn't sleep. |
| **Scene** | The player asks what Thera wants. For herself. Not for others. |
| **The dream** | "A place like this. But... mine. Quiet mornings. Someone who stays. Time to just... be." |
| **Key moment** | Thera has never said this out loud. |

**Why this matters**: When the sacrifice comes, we know exactly what she's giving up.

### Phase 10: The Truth Together - "The Roots Speak"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | The crisis is escalating. They need real answers. |
| **Scene** | They go to the Deep. Thera listens in a way she's never let herself. |
| **Revelation** | The Grove is a ship. They're arriving. Something must be given or everything dies. |
| **Key moment** | Thera goes pale. "I always knew. I didn't want to know." |

### Phase 11: The Choice - "It Has to Be Me"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | The Elders confirm. A Heart is needed. |
| **Scene** | Thera understands. Her whole life led here. |
| **The player's attempt** | Can the player volunteer instead? (No. The ship chooses. It has to be a Rootspeaker.) |
| **Thera's response** | "You've given me more than anyone ever has. Let me give something too." |

### Phase 12: The Sacrifice - "The Heart"

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Setup** | The landing is imminent. Thera goes to the heart of the ship. The player goes with her. |
| **The space** | A vast chamber of woven roots, pulsing with light. |
| **Thera's state** | Strangely calm. The player doesn't understand why she's not more afraid. |

## The Sacrifice Scene

**What the PLAYER experiences (Grief):**

*Thera places her hands on the central root. The roots respond, embracing her. She begins to glow.*

> "All my life, I listened. To the sick, the grieving, the Grove itself. You were the first voice that listened back."

*The light intensifies. She's being pulled in. The player reaches for her - but can't hold on.*

> "I used to be so afraid of this moment. But I looked for myself, and I couldn't find her. Isn't that strange?"

*She smiles - peaceful, almost amused - and the player doesn't understand why.*

> "Just remember this: if you ever look for me and can't find me... that might not be as sad as it sounds."

*The light takes her completely. Silence.*

*She's gone.*

## Thera's Spiritual Arc: The Carrying Heart Awakens

> **Note for Designers:** This section documents the Buddhist source material underlying the Three Roots. Technical terms here (arhat, bodhisattva, samsara) should NEVER appear in game content - only their in-world equivalents.

### The Lamrim Framework

| Scope | Motivation | In-World Term |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| **Initial** | Seeking safety, belonging | First Root: Finding Ground |
| **Medium** | Wanting to escape suffering | Second Root: The Yearning |
| **Great** | Taking responsibility for all beings | Third Root: Remaining |

### The Key Dramatic Insight

Both paths (rest and remaining) realize the same emptiness. The difference is what you DO with it:
- Emptiness alone → Rest (the valid arhat path - Seren's choice)
- Emptiness + love for beings → Remaining (the bodhisattva path - Lira's choice)

### How This Maps to Thera's Arc

| Act | Scope | What Thera Develops | In-World Term |
|-----|-------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Act 1** | Initial | Seeking safety, belonging, identity | "Finding Root" (First Root) |
| **Act 2** | Medium (Renunciation) | Seeing suffering clearly, wanting to escape | "The Yearning" (Second Root) |
| **Act 3** | Medium → Emptiness | Understanding no-self (the Hollow Root) | "Seeing Through" |
| **Act 3-4** | Great (Bodhicitta) | Love for all beings arises | "The Carrying Heart wakes" |
| **Act 4** | Union | Emptiness + Bodhicitta fuse | "The Heart Opens Through" |
| **Fusion** | First Bhumi | Joy - the breakthrough | "The First Ground" |

### The Critical Distinction: Two Valid Paths

| Path | What It Means | Result |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Arhat Path** | Emptiness understood, used for personal liberation | REST - dissolving into peace |
| **Bodhisattva Path** | Emptiness + Bodhicitta, used for all beings | REMAINING - staying awake to carry others |

**Lira chose the Third Root.** She could have dissolved. She had realized emptiness. But love for the beings who would come after prevented her from resting.

**Thera must make the same choice.** Not between good and bad - between two kinds of liberation.

### The Moment of Fusion

**What THERA experiences (hidden from player until later):**
- She understands the Hollow Root (no self to protect)
- The Carrying Heart arises (love without edges)
- They unite - and she LAUGHS
- Not because she was making peace with death
- But because she discovered there was never anyone to die
- Just love, moving through a shape, now free to move through everything
- JOY - vast, settled, delighted

**What the PLAYER experiences:**
- Grief. Pure, sacred grief.
- Thera's strange calmness seems like acceptance of death
- Her final words are cryptic but peaceful
- The player doesn't understand yet

### The Joy Revelation (Slow)

The truth emerges over subsequent gameplay:
- Strange warmth in the Pulse
- Flowers blooming where Thera used to sit
- Dreams where she appears, not sad but joyful
- Finally: direct contact, and understanding

**The grief transforms. But not quickly. Not easily. And that's what makes it sacred.**

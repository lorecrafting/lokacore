# Seedship Forest - Story

> This file covers the 108-beat structure, the four acts, the woven threads, conflicts, and seeds/payoffs.

---

## Content Structure Overview

The story is told in **4 Acts**, with main story phases as the spine and side content as the flesh.

| Act | Main Phases | Side Content | Playtime Target |
|-----|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Act 1: Arrival** | 1-2 | 8-12 quests + moments | 3-5 hours |
| **Act 2: Belonging** | 3-5 | 12-18 quests + moments | 5-8 hours |
| **Act 3: Discovery** | 6-8 | 8-12 quests + moments | 4-6 hours |
| **Act 4: Crisis** | 9-12 | 4-6 quests + moments | 3-4 hours |

---

## The Three Roots (Hidden Thematic Structure)

The story encodes a progression through three stages of spiritual development, never named explicitly but woven through the narrative.

| Root | Act | Player Experience | Character Model | Core Question |
|------|-----|-------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **First Root: Finding Ground** | Act 1 | Seeking safety, belonging, learning the rules | All Tenders - they tend because it gives them purpose | "How do I survive? How do I belong?" |
| **Second Root: The Yearning** | Act 2 | Seeing suffering clearly, feeling the weight, dreaming of escape | Thera's exhaustion, her nightmares of dissolution | "How do I escape this weight? Is rest possible?" |
| **Third Root: Remaining** | Acts 3-4 | Understanding that rest isn't the answer, choosing to carry others | Lira's 200-year vigil, Thera's final choice | "How do I carry others home? Can I stay?" |

**The Teaching Itself** (discovered in Act 2 side quest):

> *First Root*: "The seed seeks soil. It grows for itself. There is no shame in seeking shelter."
>
> *Second Root*: "The tree dreams of becoming soil again. Quiet. Dark. Still. Many stop here, believing rest is the end of suffering."
>
> *Third Root*: "The roots hold the ground for others. The branches shelter those below. Not rest. Not reaching. Remaining."

## Conflict & Resolution Map

### The Major Conflicts

| Conflict | Parties | Core Tension | Resolution |
|----------|---------|--------------|------------|
| **Player vs. Thera** | "Let me help" vs. "Let me rest" | Player wants in, Thera guards herself | She lets player see her |

## Side Quests (Key Examples)

### The Three Roots (Major Thematic Quest)

**Giver**: Tomas, during a lucid moment
**Task**: Find the ancient carving in the Deep
**Location**: Sacred grove, on stone half-covered by roots

**The Carving:**

*First inscription:*
> "The seed falls. It seeks soil, water, light. It grows for itself. This is the First Root - finding ground."

*Second inscription:*
> "The tree grows tall. It feels the weight of sky. It dreams of becoming soil again - quiet, dark, still. This is the Second Root - the yearning for rest."

*Third inscription:*
> "But some trees understand. The roots are not for the tree. They hold the ground so others may grow. This is the Third Root - becoming the ground itself. Not rest. Not reaching. Remaining."

**Thera's Reaction**: "I've dreamed of the second. So many times. Just... stopping."

## Translation to Game Mechanics

The 108 beats map directly to game content:

| Narrative Element | Game Mechanic |
|-------------------|---------------|
| **Story beats** | Quest objectives with progression flags |
| **Dialogues** | Dialogue YAML with conditions |
| **Room state changes** | Room description variants |
| **NPC reactions** | Emote changes and schedule overrides |
| **Teaching sessions** | Multi-part quest chains |
| **The sacrifice** | Scripted sequence with cutscene |

The narrative IS the implementation spec. Each beat has:
- A location (room)
- Characters present (NPCs)
- What happens (trigger/event)
- What changes (flags, states)

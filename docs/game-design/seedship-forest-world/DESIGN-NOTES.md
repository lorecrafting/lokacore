## 2026-02-01 - Session: Dong Han Medical Integration

**Source Material:** Dong Han (Eastern Scholars) medical lecture series - traditional Daoist medicine teachings on energy, pulse diagnosis, and healing philosophy.

**Key Concepts Integrated:**

1. **The Six Qi Energies (Liu Qi)**
   - Wind, Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Fire, Cold
   - Now form the hidden pattern of Blight progression
   - Pulse Qualities table updated with Six Qi correspondences
   - NPCs can teach this through seasonal disease metaphor

2. **The 70/30 Coexistence Rule**
   - "Never satisfy 100%; leave room for the body's own healing"
   - Added to Tender's Healing Philosophy section
   - Integrated into Phase 4 Session 2 teaching
   - Spark embodies this wisdom in dialogue

3. **The Synchronization Principle**
   - "To manipulate energy, you must first synchronize with it"
   - Added to Phase 4 Session 1 teaching
   - Player must "sync" before taking pulse

4. **Tonification Over Sedation**
   - "Strengthen the body rather than fight the pathogen"
   - Frames Thera's sacrifice as joining/supporting, not fixing
   - Remedies in gameplay add support, don't attack

5. **The Nine Sectors (Ship Anatomy)**
   - Three Jiao (Upper/Middle/Lower) × Three Positions (Head/Body/Tail)
   - Creates diagnostic map for Rootspeakers
   - Thera's final journey visits key sectors

6. **Hidden Pulse (Tomas)**
   - "Hidden Yang within Yin" - one energy peeking through another
   - Explains Tomas's lucid moments as Lira speaking through him
   - New dialogue added for his condition

7. **Spark Pulse-Sensing System**
   - Progressive diagnostic ability through story
   - Full Six Qi reading by Act 4
   - Embodies 70/30 wisdom in dialogue

**Design Principle Maintained:**
All concepts are encoded through felt experience and NPC dialogue. No explicit naming of Six Qi, Dong Han, or medical terms. Players learn by DOING, not by being told.

## 2026-01-31 - Session 1

**Key Decisions:**
- Monastery was placeholder, starting fresh
- Focus on ONE world first
- No content lockout (always-on MMO)
- Want to "draw them in" emotionally

**The Concept:**
- Players start in what appears to be a forest
- Gradually discover they're inside a giant seedship
- "Space druids" vibe without calling them druids

**Cosmology Implications:**
- Souls can evolve into planetary beings
- The seedships carry souls that will become planets
- Every planet may have started with a sacrifice like this

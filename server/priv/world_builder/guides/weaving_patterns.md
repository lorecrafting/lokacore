# Weaving Patterns

How to create an interconnected narrative web that makes stories feel alive.

## The Six Thread Types

### 1. Hidden Stories

Parallel narratives revealed gradually through fragments.

**Example**: The elder's lost love
- Beat 12: Photo on shelf, face turned away
- Beat 34: Old letter found, no signature
- Beat 56: Another NPC mentions "the one who left"
- Beat 78: Full story revealed

**Pattern**: Fragment → Fragment → Fragment → Whole

### 2. Physical Artifacts

Objects that carry meaning across the story.

**Example**: A worn pendant
- Beat 8: Player notices NPC touching pendant nervously
- Beat 25: Same pendant seen in old painting
- Beat 45: Found broken on the ground after crisis
- Beat 89: Player must decide what to do with it

**Pattern**: Notice → Context → Crisis → Resolution

### 3. Echoing Phrases

Lines said once, repeated later with new meaning.

**Example**: "The forest provides"
- Beat 5: Villager says it casually
- Beat 30: Said bitterly after loss
- Beat 60: Player discovers why forest "provides"
- Beat 95: Player chooses to say it (or not)

**Pattern**: Establish → Subvert → Reveal → Transform

### 4. Visual/Sensory Echoes

Details that recur meaningfully.

**Example**: The smell of burning leaves
- Beat 3: Pleasant autumn atmosphere
- Beat 40: Triggers NPC's traumatic memory
- Beat 70: Player understands the connection
- Beat 100: Same smell, completely different meaning

**Pattern**: Neutral → Charged → Explained → Transformed

### 5. Mirror Moments

Scenes that rhyme with each other.

**Example**: Two farewells
- Beat 15: Companion leaves player to "check something"
- Beat 85: Player must leave companion for the same reason
- The callback creates emotional resonance

**Pattern**: Establish situation → Echo situation with role reversal

### 6. Character Revelations

Backstories revealed in stages.

**Example**: Why the healer left the city
- Beat 10: She deflects questions about her past
- Beat 35: Scars visible when treating wound
- Beat 55: Old colleague arrives, tension evident
- Beat 75: Full story in moment of vulnerability

**Pattern**: Mystery → Hints → Pressure → Revelation

## Threading Rules

### The Rule of Three

Every thread needs at least 3 touchpoints:
1. **Plant**: Introduce element (can be subtle)
2. **Develop**: Build on it, add context
3. **Payoff**: Resolution or transformation

Threads with only 1-2 touchpoints feel abandoned.

### Spacing Guidelines

| Story Length | Minimum Space Between Touchpoints |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| 30 beats | 5-8 beats |
| 60 beats | 10-15 beats |
| 108 beats | 15-25 beats |

Too close = obvious. Too far = forgotten.

### Thread Density

| Story Length | Recommended Threads |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Short (30) | 3-5 threads |
| Medium (60) | 6-10 threads |
| Long (108) | 12-18 threads |

More threads = richer tapestry, but harder to track.

## Weaving Techniques

### The Convergence

Multiple threads meet at a single moment:
- Beat 78: The pendant (artifact), the phrase (echo), and the hidden story all connect at once
- Creates powerful "aha" moment
- Use sparingly (2-3 times per long story)

### The Handoff

One thread leads into another:
- Investigating the pendant leads to discovering the hidden story
- Creates natural progression
- Reduces "why am I doing this?" feelings

### The Parallel

Two threads develop simultaneously, contrasting each other:
- Thread A: Elder finding peace with loss
- Thread B: Player uncovering what was lost
- Creates dramatic irony
- Enriches both threads

### The Reversal

Thread payoff inverts expectation:
- Established: The artifact seems cursed
- Revealed: It was protecting someone
- Creates satisfying surprise
- Must be fair (clues planted)

## Common Problems

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Orphaned plant | Element introduced but never developed | Add touchpoints or remove |
| Payoff without setup | Resolution feels unearned | Plant seeds earlier |
| Obvious threading | Player predicts everything | Add misdirection |
| Thread overload | Story feels cluttered | Combine similar threads |
| Uneven spacing | Some threads feel rushed | Redistribute touchpoints |

## Thread Tracking Template

For each thread, document:

```
Thread Name: [Name]
Type: [Hidden Story / Artifact / Phrase / etc.]

Plant:
- Beat: [#]
- What happens: [Description]

Development:
- Beat: [#] - [What happens]
- Beat: [#] - [What happens]

Payoff:
- Beat: [#]
- What happens: [Description]
- Emotional impact: [What player feels]

Connections to other threads: [List]
```

## Quality Checklist

Before marking threads complete:

- [ ] Every thread has 3+ touchpoints
- [ ] Plants are subtle but noticeable on replay
- [ ] Payoffs feel earned, not contrived
- [ ] Spacing feels natural
- [ ] Convergence points are powerful
- [ ] No orphaned elements
- [ ] Thread count is manageable

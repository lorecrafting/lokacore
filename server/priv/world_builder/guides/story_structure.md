# Story Structure

How to structure narrative arcs in game worlds.

## The 108 Beat Structure

108 is a meaningful number in many traditions (Buddhist mala beads). Use it or another number meaningful to your source philosophy.

Each beat = one scene, moment, or revelation.

### Act Structure

| Act | Beats | Name | Purpose |
|-----|-------|------|---------|
| Act 1 | 1-27 | Finding Ground | Player arrives, learns the world |
| Act 2 | 28-54 | Deepening | Relationships form, mystery grows |
| Act 3 | 55-81 | Revelation | Truth emerges, stakes rise |
| Act 4 | 82-100 | Crisis | The choice, the sacrifice |
| Epilogue | 101-108 | Transformation | Grief → Joy |

### Beat Definition

For each beat, define:
1. **Action**: What physically happens
2. **Information**: What player learns
3. **Emotion**: What player feels
4. **Threads**: What narrative threads connect here

### Example Beat

```
Beat 47: The Empty Grave

ACTION: Player discovers Lira's grave is empty. The earth is loose,
recently disturbed.

INFORMATION: Lira's body was moved. When? By whom?

EMOTION: Unease. The mystery deepens. Something is wrong.

THREADS:
- Physical Artifact: The pendant (from Beat 18) should be here but isn't
- Hidden Story: Connects to Yara's nighttime wandering (Beat 32)
- Echoing Phrase: "The dead don't stay buried" (first heard Beat 15)
```

## Pacing Guidelines

### Tension Curve

Track tension level (1-10) across beats:

```
10 |                              *****
 8 |                         ****     ***
 6 |               ****  ****             **
 4 |        *** ***    **                   **
 2 | *** ***                                  ****
   +------------------------------------------
     Act 1      Act 2      Act 3    Act 4   Epilogue
```

### Valleys Are Essential

Don't maintain constant high tension. Include:
- Quiet moments of connection
- Humor to release pressure
- Beauty that isn't threatened
- Simple pleasures

These make the peaks hit harder.

### Escalation Pattern

Each act should escalate:
- **Stakes**: What's at risk increases
- **Understanding**: Player knows more
- **Complexity**: Simple becomes nuanced
- **Emotional investment**: Characters matter more

## Quest vs. Story Beats

| Quest Beat | Story Beat |
|------------|------------|
| Task-focused | Character-focused |
| "Go here, do this" | "This changes how you see things" |
| Can be skipped | Essential to understanding |
| Mechanical reward | Emotional reward |

**Best quests serve both**: The task reveals character or advances the mystery.

## Revelation Pacing

Don't reveal everything at once. Layer revelations:

```
Beat 20: Something is strange about this forest
Beat 35: The "natural" formations are too regular
Beat 50: Player finds a fragment of metal in the soil
Beat 65: The metal is clearly manufactured
Beat 80: The forest IS the ship
Beat 95: The ship still has a mission
```

Each revelation reframes what came before.

## The Emotional Arc

### Act 1: Wonder → Belonging
Player arrives as outsider, finds place in community

### Act 2: Belonging → Doubt
Something doesn't add up. Questions emerge.

### Act 3: Doubt → Understanding
Truth emerges, but what does it mean?

### Act 4: Understanding → Sacrifice
What must be given up? What preserved?

### Epilogue: Grief → Joy
Loss is real. But meaning remains. A bittersweet completion.

## Common Structural Problems

| Problem | Sign | Fix |
|---------|------|-----|
| Saggy middle | Act 2 feels slow | Add subplot complications |
| Rushed ending | Too much happens at once | Spread revelations earlier |
| Anticlimactic | Crisis doesn't feel earned | Raise stakes throughout |
| Unearned emotion | Grief/joy feels forced | Build relationships first |
| Deus ex machina | Solution appears from nowhere | Plant seeds early |

## MUD-Specific Considerations

### Player Agency
- Major revelations should require player action to trigger
- Let players discover at their own pace
- Provide multiple paths to the same truth

### Replayability
- Side content reveals different facets
- Character relationships unlock new dialogue
- Some truths only emerge on second playthrough

### Session Boundaries
- Each play session should have a mini-arc
- End sessions on hooks, not cliffs
- Provide natural stopping points
